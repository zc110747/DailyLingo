# The Lost Puppy

## Article

It is Monday afternoon. Ben is walking home from school when he sees a small dog near a tree.

The dog is brown and white. It looks tired and a little afraid. Ben looks around, but he cannot see its owner.

“Are you lost?” Ben asks.

The dog looks at him and moves its tail.

Ben takes a bottle of water from his bag. He pours some water into a small cup and puts it on the ground. The dog drinks the water quickly.

Ben wants to help the dog find its home. He sees a phone number on the dog's collar. He calls the number and talks to a woman.

Ten minutes later, the woman arrives. She is very happy to see her dog.

“Thank you for helping my little dog,” she says.

Ben smiles and continues his walk home. He feels good because he has helped an animal in need.

## Questions

Question 1：When does Ben see the dog?

A. On Monday afternoon.

B. On Sunday morning.

C. On Friday evening.

Question 2：What color is the dog?

A. Black and gray.

B. Brown and white.

C. Yellow and brown.

Question 3：What does Ben give the dog?

A. Some food.

B. A toy.

C. Some water.

Question 4：How does Ben find the dog's owner?

A. He sees her near the tree.

B. He calls the phone number on the dog's collar.

C. He asks his teacher for help.

Question 5：How does Ben feel at the end of the story?

A. Happy because he helps the dog.

B. Angry because he is late.

C. Afraid of the dog.

## Answers

1\. **A. On Monday afternoon.** Ben sees the dog while he is walking home from school on Monday afternoon.

2\. **B. Brown and white.** The dog has brown and white fur.

3\. **C. Some water.** Ben pours water into a small cup for the thirsty dog.

4\. **B. He calls the phone number on the dog's collar.** Ben finds a phone number on the collar and calls it to contact the owner.

5\. **A. Happy because he helps the dog.** Ben feels good because he helps the lost dog return to its owner.
