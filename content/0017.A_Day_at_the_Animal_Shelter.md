# A Day at the Animal Shelter

## Article

Lucy loves animals. One Saturday morning, she visits an animal shelter with her father.

There are many animals in the shelter. Lucy sees small cats, big dogs, and two little rabbits. Some animals sleep, and others play in the garden.

A small brown dog comes to Lucy. It has long ears and a short tail. The dog looks at her and moves its tail.

“Hello, little dog!” Lucy says. She gives the dog some food and fresh water.

Then Lucy helps her father clean the animal cages. She also plays with a white cat. The cat jumps onto a chair and closes its eyes.

Before they leave, Lucy asks her father, “Can we take one animal home?”

Her father smiles. “We can talk about it with your mother.”

Lucy is happy. She wants to help the animals again next weekend.

## Questions

**Question 1：Who visits the animal shelter with Lucy?**

A. Her mother.

B. Her father.

C. Her friend.

**Question 2：Which animals does Lucy see?**

A. Cats, dogs, and rabbits.

B. Lions, tigers, and bears.

C. Ducks, chickens, and horses.

**Question 3：What does Lucy give the small dog?**

A. A toy and a ball.

B. Milk and bread.

C. Food and fresh water.

**Question 4：Where does the white cat jump?**

A. Onto a chair.

B. Onto a table.

C. Into a box.

**Question 5：When does Lucy want to help the animals again?**

A. Tomorrow morning.

B. Next weekend.

C. Next month.

## Answers

1\. **B. Her father.** Lucy visits the animal shelter with her father.

2\. **A. Cats, dogs, and rabbits.** She sees these animals at the shelter.

3\. **C. Food and fresh water.** Lucy gives the small dog food and fresh water.

4\. **A. Onto a chair.** The white cat jumps onto a chair and closes its eyes.

5\. **B. Next weekend.** Lucy wants to visit and help the animals again next weekend.
