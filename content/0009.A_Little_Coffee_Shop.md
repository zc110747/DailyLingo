
# A Little Coffee Shop

## Article

There is a small coffee shop on Green Street. It is next to a flower shop and across from a bus stop.

The coffee shop opens at seven o'clock every morning. It sells coffee, tea, milk, and fresh bread.

The shop has four small tables and eight chairs. There are flowers on the tables, and the walls are light yellow.

A cup of coffee costs three dollars. A cup of tea costs two dollars. People can also buy a sandwich for four dollars.

Many people come here before work. They sit by the window, drink their coffee, and read the news.

The owner is a friendly woman named Anna. She always smiles and says hello to her customers.

The coffee shop is small, but many people like it because it is warm and comfortable.

## Questions

Question 1：Where is the coffee shop?

A. Next to a flower shop.

B. Inside a school.

C. Behind a hospital.

Question 2：What time does the coffee shop open?

A. At six o'clock.

B. At seven o'clock.

C. At eight o'clock.

Question 3：How much is a cup of tea?

A. Two dollars.

B. Three dollars.

C. Four dollars.

Question 4：What do people often do at the coffee shop before work?

A. Play football.

B. Buy new clothes.

C. Drink coffee and read the news.

Question 5：Why do many people like the coffee shop?

A. It is very large.

B. It is warm and comfortable.

C. It is open all night.

## Answers

1\. **A. Next to a flower shop.** The coffee shop is next to a flower shop and across from a bus stop.

2\. **B. At seven o'clock.** The shop opens at seven o'clock every morning.

3\. **A. Two dollars.** A cup of tea costs two dollars.

4\. **C. Drink coffee and read the news.** Many people visit the shop before work to enjoy their drinks and read the news.

5\. **B. It is warm and comfortable.** Although the coffee shop is small, people enjoy its warm and comfortable atmosphere.
