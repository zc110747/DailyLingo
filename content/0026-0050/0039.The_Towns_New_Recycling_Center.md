# The Town's New Recycling Center

## Article

A new recycling center opens in Daniel's town this week. It is next to the public park, and everyone can visit it for free.

On Saturday morning, Daniel goes there with his mother. At the entrance, a friendly worker gives them a small map.

“Welcome!” she says. “Here, you can learn how to recycle things and help keep our town clean.”

Daniel walks into the first room. He sees three large boxes. One is for paper, one is for plastic, and one is for metal.

His mother shows him an empty bottle.

“Which box should we use for this bottle?” she asks.

Daniel looks at the signs. “The plastic box!” he answers.

“That's right,” his mother says.

In the next room, Daniel sees some old toys and books. The center gives these things to families who need them. People can bring their clean, useful items here instead of throwing them away.

Daniel likes this idea. He has some old books at home, so he decides to bring them next time.

Before leaving, Daniel plants a small flower near the center with a group of children. They water the flowers and pick up some paper from the ground.

On the way home, Daniel says, “I didn't know we could do so many things to help the environment.”

His mother smiles. “Every small action can make a difference.”

Daniel decides to recycle more at home and remind his family to use less plastic.

## Questions

**Question 1: When does Daniel visit the recycling center?**

A. On Saturday morning.

B. On Sunday night.

C. On Monday afternoon.

**Question 2: What is one of the large boxes for?**

A. Food.

B. Plastic.

C. Clothes.

**Question 3: What can people do with old books and toys at the center?**

A. Sell them to a shop.

B. Leave them in the park.

C. Give them to families who need them.

**Question 4: What does Daniel do before leaving?**

A. He plants a flower.

B. He buys a new toy.

C. He paints a wall.

**Question 5: What does Daniel decide to do at home?**

A. Use more plastic.

B. Recycle more and use less plastic.

C. Throw all his old books away.

## Answers

1. **A. On Saturday morning.** Daniel visits the new recycling center with his mother on Saturday morning.

2. **B. Plastic.** One of the three boxes is for plastic.

3. **C. Give them to families who need them.** The center collects useful items and gives them to other families.

4. **A. He plants a flower.** Daniel joins a group of children to plant a flower and help clean the area.

5. **B. Recycle more and use less plastic.** Daniel wants to take small actions to help the environment.
