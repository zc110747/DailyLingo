# A Poster in the Town

## Article

One morning, Emma sees a colorful poster near the bus stop. It is about a book festival in the town library.

The festival is on Saturday. There are many activities for children and adults. People can read books, listen to stories, and meet local writers.

Emma likes reading, so she takes a photo of the poster. She sends it to her friend Jack.

“Would you like to go with me?” she asks.

“Of course!” Jack replies. “I want to find a book about space.”

On Saturday morning, they meet outside the library. The building is full of people. A woman reads a funny story to a group of children. A man talks about his new book.

Emma finds a book about animals, and Jack finds a book about the stars. They both buy their books and sit in the garden to read.

Before they leave, they see a small sign. It says, “Books can open new doors.”

Emma smiles. She thinks it is a good message. A book can take people to new places without a ticket.

## Questions

**Question 1: Where does Emma see the poster?**

A. Near the bus stop.

B. Inside a supermarket.

C. At her school.

**Question 2: When is the book festival?**

A. On Friday.

B. On Saturday.

C. On Sunday.

**Question 3: What kind of book does Jack want to find?**

A. A book about cooking.

B. A book about animals.

C. A book about space.

**Question 4: What does Emma buy?**

A. A book about animals.

B. A book about cars.

C. A book about music.

**Question 5: What does Emma think about the message on the sign?**

A. It is a good message.

B. It is difficult to understand.

C. It is not interesting.

## Answers

1. **A. Near the bus stop.** Emma sees the colorful poster near the bus stop one morning.

2. **B. On Saturday.** The poster says that the book festival is on Saturday.

3. **C. A book about space.** Jack tells Emma that he wants to find a book about space.

4. **A. A book about animals.** Emma finds and buys a book about animals.

5. **A. It is a good message.** Emma thinks books can take people to new places through reading.
