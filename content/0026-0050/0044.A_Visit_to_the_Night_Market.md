# A Visit to the Night Market

## Article

On Friday evening, Leo visits a night market with his parents. The market is in a busy street near their home.

When they arrive, Leo sees many small shops. Some people sell clothes, toys, and bags. Other people sell hot food and cold drinks.

A shop near the entrance sells colorful paper fans. Leo chooses a blue fan for his grandmother.

Then he smells something sweet. A food seller is making small cakes on a hot plate. Leo asks his father if he can try one.

“Of course,” his father says.

The cakes are warm and soft. Leo likes them very much.

After that, the family walks to a fruit stand. His mother buys some oranges and bananas. The seller puts the fruit in a paper bag.

Near the end of the street, a musician is playing a small drum. A few children stop to listen. Leo claps his hands with them.

Before going home, Leo buys a bottle of water. The market is crowded, and he is a little tired.

On the way home, he tells his parents about his favorite things at the market.

“I like the cakes best,” he says.

His mother laughs. “Next time, we can come back and try something new.”

Leo smiles. He enjoys the night market because there is always something to see, hear, and taste.

## Questions

**Question 1: When does Leo visit the night market?**

A. On Friday evening.

B. On Monday morning.

C. On Sunday afternoon.

**Question 2: What does Leo buy for his grandmother?**

A. A red bag.

B. A blue paper fan.

C. A pair of shoes.

**Question 3: What food does Leo try?**

A. A bowl of soup.

B. A piece of pizza.

C. A small cake.

**Question 4: What does Leo's mother buy?**

A. Oranges and bananas.

B. Apples and grapes.

C. Bread and eggs.

**Question 5: Why does Leo enjoy the night market?**

A. It is always quiet.

B. There are many things to see, hear, and taste.

C. He can play football there.

## Answers

1. **A. On Friday evening.** Leo visits the night market with his parents after the day ends.

2. **B. A blue paper fan.** He chooses a blue fan as a gift for his grandmother.

3. **C. A small cake.** Leo tries a warm, soft cake from a food seller.

4. **A. Oranges and bananas.** His mother buys these fruits at a fruit stand.

5. **B. There are many things to see, hear, and taste.** Leo enjoys the shops, food, and music at the market.
