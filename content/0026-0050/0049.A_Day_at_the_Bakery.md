# A Day at the Bakery

## Article

Tom works at a small bakery with his mother. They get up early every morning and make fresh bread.

Today, a young girl comes into the shop. She wants a cake for her father's birthday. Tom shows her three cakes. One is big and white, one is small and pink, and one has chocolate on top.

The girl likes the chocolate cake. Tom puts it in a box and adds a small card. The girl writes, “Happy Birthday, Dad!” on the card.

Before she leaves, she buys two warm pieces of bread. She says thank you and smiles.

Tom feels happy. He likes making food for people. Every day, he meets new people and learns something new.

## Questions

**Question 1: Who works with Tom at the bakery?**

A. His mother.

B. His teacher.

C. His friend.

**Question 2: Why does the girl buy a cake?**

A. She wants to eat it at school.

B. It is her father's birthday.

C. She wants to give it to Tom.

**Question 3: What does the girl buy before she leaves?**

A. Two warm pieces of bread.

B. Three cups of tea.

C. A new camera.

## Answers

1\. **A. His mother.** Tom works at the bakery with his mother.

2\. **B. It is her father's birthday.** The girl wants a cake for her father's special day.

3\. **A. Two warm pieces of bread.** She buys two pieces of bread before leaving the shop.
