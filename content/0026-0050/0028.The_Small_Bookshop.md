# The Small Bookshop

## Article

There is a small bookshop near Mary's home. It is between a bakery and a flower shop. Mary walks past it every day, but she never goes inside.

One rainy afternoon, Mary has nothing to do. She takes an umbrella and walks to the bookshop.

A little bell rings when she opens the door. The shop is warm and quiet. There are books on the shelves, and a small cat sleeps near the window.

An old man stands behind the counter. He smiles at Mary.

“Hello! Can I help you find a book?” he asks.

Mary likes stories about animals. The man shows her a book about a little fox. Mary reads the first page and likes it very much.

“How much is this book?” she asks.

“It is five dollars,” the man answers.

Mary buys the book and sits in a small chair near the window. She reads for half an hour. When the rain stops, she says goodbye to the man and goes home.

From that day on, Mary visits the bookshop every week. It becomes one of her favorite places.

## Answers

1\. **A. Near Mary's home.** The bookshop is close to Mary's home, between a bakery and a flower shop.

2\. **B. She has nothing to do on a rainy afternoon.** Mary decides to visit the bookshop because she has free time.

3\. **A. A little fox.** Mary chooses a story about a little fox.

4\. **C. Five dollars.** The shopkeeper tells Mary that the book costs five dollars.

5\. **B. Every week.** Mary likes the bookshop and begins visiting it every week.
