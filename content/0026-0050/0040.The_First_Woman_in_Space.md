# The First Woman in Space

## Article

Many years ago, people wanted to know more about space. They built rockets and sent people into space.

In 1963, a woman named Valentina Tereshkova flew into space. She came from the Soviet Union. She was the first woman to travel into space.

Valentina was born in 1937. When she was young, she worked in a factory. She also liked jumping from planes with a parachute.

Later, she joined a space program. She trained hard and learned many new things. At first, space travel was difficult and dangerous. People needed to prepare carefully.

On June 16, 1963, Valentina went into space in a spacecraft called Vostok 6. She traveled around the Earth many times during her flight.

Her journey lasted almost three days. When she returned to Earth, many people knew her name.

Today, people still remember Valentina as an important person in space history. Her journey showed that women could also take part in space exploration.

Some children dream of becoming astronauts when they grow up. They look at the stars and imagine traveling far above the Earth.

Valentina's story reminds us that learning, training, and courage can help people do difficult things.

## Questions

**Question 1: Who was the first woman to travel into space?**

A. Valentina Tereshkova.

B. Marie Curie.

C. Amelia Earhart.

**Question 2: In which year did Valentina travel into space?**

A. 1937.

B. 1963.

C. 1973.

**Question 3: What did Valentina like doing when she was young?**

A. Sailing boats.

B. Playing the piano.

C. Jumping from planes with a parachute.

**Question 4: What was the name of her spacecraft?**

A. Apollo 11.

B. Vostok 6.

C. Voyager 1.

**Question 5: What does Valentina's story teach us?**

A. Space travel is easy for everyone.

B. People should never try difficult things.

C. Learning, training, and courage can help people do difficult things.

## Answers

1. **A. Valentina Tereshkova.** She became the first woman to travel into space in 1963.

2. **B. 1963.** Her spaceflight took place on June 16, 1963.

3. **C. Jumping from planes with a parachute.** Before joining the space program, she enjoyed parachuting.

4. **B. Vostok 6.** Vostok 6 was the spacecraft she used for her historic journey.

5. **C. Learning, training, and courage can help people do difficult things.** Her journey required preparation and courage.
