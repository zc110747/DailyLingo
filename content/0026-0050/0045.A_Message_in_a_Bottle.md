# A Message in a Bottle

## Article

One sunny morning, a girl named Ruby walks along the beach with her dog, Max. The sea is blue, and small waves move across the sand.

Max runs near the water. Suddenly, he stops beside a rock and barks.

Ruby walks over to see what he has found. There is a glass bottle on the sand. Inside the bottle, she can see a small piece of paper.

Ruby carefully opens the bottle and takes out the paper. It has a short message on it.

“Hello! My name is Ben. I live near the sea. I put this message in the bottle last summer. I hope someone finds it one day!”

Ruby smiles. She thinks the message is interesting. She wonders where Ben lives and how far the bottle has traveled.

When Ruby gets home, she tells her father about the bottle. He helps her look for more information on the paper, but there is no address.

“Maybe we can write a reply and put it in another bottle,” Ruby says.

Her father shakes his head gently. “Glass bottles can be dangerous for people and animals in the sea. Let's find a better way to contact him.”

Ruby agrees. They take a photo of the message and share it on a local community website.

Two days later, Ruby receives a message from Ben. He lives in a town on the other side of the bay. He is happy that someone has found his note.

Ruby learns that a small message can connect people, even when they live far apart. She also learns that it is important to protect the sea and the animals that live there.

## Questions

**Question 1: Who walks along the beach with Ruby?**

A. Her brother.

B. Her dog, Max.

C. Her teacher.

**Question 2: What does Ruby find inside the bottle?**

A. A small piece of paper.

B. A gold coin.

C. A little fish.

**Question 3: When did Ben put the message in the bottle?**

A. Yesterday morning.

B. Two days ago.

C. Last summer.

**Question 4: How does Ruby help Ben find her?**

A. She puts another bottle in the sea.

B. She shares a photo of the message on a community website.

C. She sends a letter to every town.

**Question 5: What does Ruby learn from this experience?**

A. People should leave glass bottles on beaches.

B. Messages can connect people, and the sea needs protection.

C. It is impossible to contact people in other towns.

## Answers

1. **B. Her dog, Max.** Ruby is walking along the beach with her dog when he finds the bottle.

2. **A. A small piece of paper.** Ruby sees a piece of paper inside the glass bottle.

3. **C. Last summer.** Ben writes that he put the message in the bottle last summer.

4. **B. She shares a photo of the message on a community website.** This helps Ben see the message and contact Ruby.

5. **B. Messages can connect people, and the sea needs protection.** Ruby connects with Ben while also learning not to leave dangerous glass in the sea.
