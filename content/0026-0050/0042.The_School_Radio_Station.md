# The School Radio Station

## Article

Every Wednesday, a group of students makes a radio show at their school. They share news, play music, and talk about school life.

Today, it is Amy's first day as a radio host. She feels a little nervous, but her friend Ben is there to help her.

At nine o'clock, they enter a small room near the library. There are two microphones, a computer, and a pair of headphones on the desk.

Ben turns on the computer and checks the sound. Amy looks at her notes.

“Are you ready?” Ben asks.

“I think so!” Amy says.

The red light turns on. It means that the show is live.

“Good morning, everyone!” Amy says into the microphone. “Welcome to our school radio show.”

First, she tells the students about a football game this Friday. Then Ben plays a popular song. After the song, Amy reads a short story about a lost cat.

At the end of the show, Amy thanks everyone for listening.

When they leave the room, their teacher is waiting outside.

“You both did a great job,” she says.

Amy is happy. She learns that speaking to many people is not as scary as she thinks.

Next week, she wants to interview a student who has won a school art prize.

She writes the idea in her notebook. There is always something interesting to share on the school radio.

## Questions

**Question 1: How often do the students make a radio show?**

A. Every day.

B. Every Wednesday.

C. Every Sunday.

**Question 2: How does Amy feel on her first day as a radio host?**

A. A little nervous.

B. Very angry.

C. Very tired.

**Question 3: What does the red light mean?**

A. The room is too hot.

B. The computer is broken.

C. The show is live.

**Question 4: What does Amy talk about first?**

A. A school trip.

B. A football game.

C. A new library.

**Question 5: What does Amy want to do next week?**

A. Interview a student who won an art prize.

B. Make a cake with Ben.

C. Visit a radio station in another town.

## Answers

1. **B. Every Wednesday.** The students make their school radio show every Wednesday.

2. **A. A little nervous.** Amy feels nervous because it is her first day as a radio host.

3. **C. The show is live.** The red light tells them that the radio show is on the air.

4. **B. A football game.** Amy first shares news about a football game happening on Friday.

5. **A. Interview a student who won an art prize.** Amy writes down this idea for the next show.
