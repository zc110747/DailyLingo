# The Missing Recipe

## Article

It is Sunday afternoon. Noah wants to make a cake for his mother's birthday. He finds a large bowl, some flour, two eggs, and a little milk in the kitchen.

But there is one problem. He cannot find the cake recipe.

Noah looks in a drawer and under a book on the table. He also checks his phone, but he does not see the recipe there.

His older sister, Emma, comes into the kitchen.

“What are you looking for?” she asks.

“I want to make a cake, but I cannot find the recipe,” Noah says.

Emma thinks for a moment. Then she remembers something. Their grandmother sent them a recipe last year. She looks through some old messages on her phone and finds it.

“Here it is!” Emma says.

The recipe says to mix the flour, eggs, milk, and sugar in a bowl. Then they need to put the mixture into a cake pan and bake it in the oven.

Noah and Emma work together. Noah mixes the ingredients, and Emma prepares the cake pan.

After some time, a sweet smell comes from the oven. The cake is ready!

They let it cool and put some fruit on top. When their mother comes home, Noah brings the cake to the table.

“Happy birthday!” he says.

Their mother smiles and gives them both a big hug.

Noah is happy. He learns that working together can make a difficult job easier.

## Questions

**Question 1: Why does Noah want to make a cake?**

A. It is his birthday.

B. It is his mother's birthday.

C. His sister asks him to make one.

**Question 2: What is Noah's problem at the beginning?**

A. He cannot find the recipe.

B. There is no oven in the kitchen.

C. He has no flour.

**Question 3: Where does Emma find the recipe?**

A. In a school book.

B. On a piece of paper under the table.

C. In an old message on her phone.

**Question 4: What does Emma do to help?**

A. She buys a cake from a shop.

B. She finds the recipe and prepares the cake pan.

C. She leaves the kitchen.

**Question 5: What does Noah learn?**

A. Cakes are easy to make alone.

B. Recipes are not important.

C. Working together can make a difficult job easier.

## Answers

1. **B. It is his mother's birthday.** Noah wants to make a special cake for his mother.

2. **A. He cannot find the recipe.** He has the ingredients but does not know where the recipe is.

3. **C. In an old message on her phone.** Emma remembers that their grandmother sent the recipe last year.

4. **B. She finds the recipe and prepares the cake pan.** Emma helps Noah find the instructions and get ready to bake.

5. **C. Working together can make a difficult job easier.** Noah and Emma share the work and make the cake together.
