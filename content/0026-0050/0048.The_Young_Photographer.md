# The Young Photographer

## Article

Emma likes taking photos. She has a small camera, and she takes it to the park every Sunday.

One morning, she sees a little bird in a tree. The bird has a yellow head and a long tail. Emma wants to take a photo, but the bird is very fast.

She waits quietly under the tree. After a few minutes, the bird comes back. Emma takes three photos. She is very happy.

At home, Emma looks at the photos with her father. One photo is very clear. Her father says, “This is a beautiful picture!”

Emma puts the photo on her wall. She wants to learn more about photography. Next Sunday, she will take her camera to the lake.

## Questions

**Question 1: What does Emma like doing?**

A. Taking photos.

B. Cooking dinner.

C. Reading books.

**Question 2: What does the bird look like?**

A. It has a blue head and short legs.

B. It has a yellow head and a long tail.

C. It has a red body and big wings.

**Question 3: Where does Emma want to go next Sunday?**

A. To the library.

B. To the supermarket.

C. To the lake.

## Answers

1\. **A. Taking photos.** Emma enjoys taking photos with her camera.

2\. **B. It has a yellow head and a long tail.** Emma sees the bird in a tree at the park.

3\. **C. To the lake.** Emma plans to take her camera to the lake next Sunday.
