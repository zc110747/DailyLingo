# The Weather Report

## Article

Every morning, a weather reporter gives the weather report on television. Today, a young reporter named Anna is ready to speak.

She stands in front of a large map. The map shows different cities and their weather.

“Good morning!” Anna says. “Today will be sunny in the south, but it will rain in the north.”

She points to a city near the sea. “It will be windy here, so please be careful when you ride your bike.”

Next, she talks about the afternoon. The sky will be cloudy, and the temperature will be lower than yesterday.

Anna also gives some advice. “Please take an umbrella if you go outside. It may rain in the evening.”

After the report, Anna returns to her desk. Her manager smiles and says, “Well done! Your report was clear and easy to understand.”

Anna feels happy. She likes her job because weather reports help people plan their day.

At lunchtime, Anna checks the weather on her phone. The sky outside is dark, and the wind is getting stronger.

She takes an umbrella from her bag before going out.

On her way to the cafe, she sees a man without an umbrella. He is looking at the clouds.

Anna smiles and says, “I think it is going to rain!”

## Questions

**Question 1: Where does Anna give the weather report?**

A. On television.

B. In a school library.

C. At a bus station.

**Question 2: What will the weather be like in the south?**

A. Snowy.

B. Sunny.

C. Very cold.

**Question 3: What does Anna say people should take if they go outside?**

A. A hat.

B. A book.

C. An umbrella.

**Question 4: Why does Anna like her job?**

A. She can travel every day.

B. Weather reports help people plan their day.

C. She does not need to talk to people.

**Question 5: What does Anna take from her bag before going out?**

A. An umbrella.

B. A bottle of milk.

C. A pair of shoes.

## Answers

1. **A. On television.** Anna gives the weather report on television in the morning.

2. **B. Sunny.** Anna says that the south will be sunny.

3. **C. An umbrella.** She advises people to take an umbrella because it may rain in the evening.

4. **B. Weather reports help people plan their day.** Anna likes helping people prepare for the weather.

5. **A. An umbrella.** Anna checks the weather and takes an umbrella because rain is possible.
