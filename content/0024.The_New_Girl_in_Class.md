# The New Girl in Class

## Article

It is Monday morning. A new girl comes to Emma's class. Her name is Sophie. She has long brown hair and a friendly smile.

The teacher says, “Class, this is Sophie. She is new here. Please help her feel welcome.”

Sophie sits next to Emma. At first, she is a little shy. She does not know anyone in the class.

At lunchtime, Emma walks over to Sophie.

“Would you like to have lunch with me?” Emma asks.

“Yes, thank you!” Sophie says.

They sit together and eat sandwiches and apples. They talk about their favorite books, music, and games. Emma learns that Sophie likes drawing animals.

After lunch, Emma shows Sophie the school library and the playground. They meet some other classmates, too.

When school ends, Sophie smiles at Emma.

“Thank you for being so kind to me,” she says.

Emma feels happy. She knows that a small act of kindness can make someone feel at home.

A. Rice and fish.

B. Cake and ice cream.

C. Sandwiches and apples.

**Question 4：What does Sophie like drawing?**

C. Houses.

**Question 5：How does Emma help Sophie?**

B. She does Sophie's homework.

C. She gives Sophie a new schoolbag.

## Answers

1\. **B. Sophie.** The new girl in Emma's class is named Sophie.

2\. **A. Next to Emma.** Sophie sits beside Emma in the classroom.

3\. **C. Sandwiches and apples.** They eat sandwiches and apples together at lunchtime.

4\. **B. Animals.** Emma learns that Sophie likes drawing animals.

5\. **A. She shows Sophie around the school.** Emma takes Sophie to the library and the playground and helps her meet other classmates.
