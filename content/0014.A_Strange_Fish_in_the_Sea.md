# A Strange Fish in the Sea

## Article

A group of scientists is studying fish near a small island. One morning, they find a very strange fish in the water.

The fish is small and has a long body. Its skin is dark blue, and its eyes are very big. It also has a small light near its head.

The scientists take some pictures of the fish. They watch it for a few minutes and write down what they see.

The fish moves slowly between the rocks. Then it swims into a dark place and disappears.

One scientist says, “I have never seen a fish like this before.”

The team sends the pictures to a science center. Other scientists help them study the fish and learn more about it.

They do not know everything about this animal yet. But they are excited because the sea is full of interesting life.

## Answers

1\. **A. Near a small island.** The scientists are studying fish in the sea near a small island.

2\. **B. Dark blue.** The fish has dark blue skin, a long body, and very big eyes.

3\. **A. They take pictures of it.** They photograph the fish and write down what they observe.

4\. **A. Into a dark place.** The fish swims between rocks and then disappears into a dark place.

5\. **C. They can learn more about an interesting animal.** The scientists are excited because the fish may help them discover more about life in the sea.
