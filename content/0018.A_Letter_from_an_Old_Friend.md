# A Letter from an Old Friend

## Article

Tom is cleaning his room on Sunday afternoon. He finds a small box under his bed. Inside the box, there are some old photos and a letter.

Tom opens the letter. It is from his old friend, Jack. They were classmates five years ago, but Jack's family moved to another city.

In the letter, Jack says, “Dear Tom, how are you? I live in a new house now. I have a little dog, and I go to a new school. I miss our time together. I hope we can meet again soon.”

Tom feels happy and excited. He wants to see Jack again.

He takes his phone and calls Jack. They talk about their schools, families, and favorite games. They also make a plan to meet next Saturday.

After the call, Tom puts the letter back into the box. He smiles because he has found an old friend again.

## Answers

1\. **A. On Sunday afternoon.** Tom is cleaning his room on Sunday afternoon.

2\. **A. Under his bed.** He finds the small box under his bed.

3\. **C. His old friend Jack.** Jack and Tom were classmates five years ago.

4\. **A. A little dog.** Jack writes that he has a little dog.

5\. **B. Next Saturday.** They make a plan to meet next Saturday.
