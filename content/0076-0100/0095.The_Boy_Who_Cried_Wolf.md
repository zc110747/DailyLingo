# The Boy Who Cried Wolf

## Article

A long time ago, a young boy watched sheep on a hill. His job was to keep the sheep safe.

One day, the boy felt bored. He wanted to have some fun. So he shouted, “Wolf! Wolf! A wolf is coming!”

The people in the village heard him. They ran up the hill to help. But there was no wolf. The boy laughed at them.

A few days later, he did it again. “Wolf! Wolf!” he shouted. The villagers came to help, but once again, there was no wolf.

One afternoon, a real wolf came to the hill. The boy was very afraid. He shouted, “Wolf! Please help me!”

The villagers heard him, but they did not come. They thought he was telling another lie. The wolf scared the sheep away.

The boy felt very sorry. He learned that people may not believe you if you often tell lies.

**Moral:** Always tell the truth. If you lie too often, people may stop trusting you.

## Questions

**Question 1: What is the boy’s job?**

A. He watches sheep.  
B. He sells food.  
C. He teaches children.  

**Question 2: Why does the boy shout “Wolf!” the first time?**

A. He sees a real wolf.  
B. He wants to have fun.  
C. He wants to go home.  

**Question 3: What do the villagers do when they hear him?**

A. They run to help.  
B. They go to sleep.  
C. They take the sheep away.  

**Question 4: What happens when the real wolf comes?**

A. The villagers help the boy.  
B. The boy catches the wolf.  
C. The villagers do not believe him.  

**Question 5: What is the moral of the story?**

A. Always tell the truth.  
B. Never watch sheep.  
C. Wolves are friendly animals.  

## Answers

1. **A. He watches sheep.** The boy keeps the sheep safe on the hill.
2. **B. He wants to have fun.** He shouts because he is bored.
3. **A. They run to help.** The villagers believe that there is a wolf.
4. **C. The villagers do not believe him.** They think he is lying again.
5. **A. Always tell the truth.** Lies can make people stop trusting you.
