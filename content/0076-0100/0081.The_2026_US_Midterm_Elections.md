# The 2026 US Midterm Elections

## Article

On November 3, 2026, people in the United States will vote in the midterm elections. This is an important political event in the country.

The United States has a president, but the president does not make all the laws alone. Congress also has an important role. It has two parts: the House of Representatives and the Senate.

In this election, all 435 seats in the House of Representatives are open for election. People will also vote for 35 seats in the Senate.

Why is this election called a midterm election? It happens in the middle of a president's four-year term. People do not vote for a new president in this election.

Before Election Day, candidates talk to voters. They explain their ideas about jobs, schools, health care, and other public issues. People can listen, read, and ask questions before they choose.

On Election Day, voters go to polling places or use other voting methods allowed in their states. After the votes are counted, the results show who will represent the people in Congress.

The election is one way people take part in their country's government.

## Answers

1. **A. November 3, 2026.** The midterm elections will take place on this date.
2. **B. Two.** Congress has the House of Representatives and the Senate.
3. **B. People vote for members of Congress.** A midterm election happens halfway through a president's term.
4. **C. Jobs, schools, and public issues.** Candidates explain their ideas about these topics.
5. **A. The election results are announced.** The results show who will represent the people.
6. **B. To let people take part in government.** Voting is one way people participate in public life.
