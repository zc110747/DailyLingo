# A Small Town Election

## Article

Many people in a small town are talking about a new election. The town needs a new mayor. Three people want the job.

One candidate is a teacher. Her name is Anna. She wants better schools and a clean town. Another candidate is a shop owner. He wants more jobs for local people. The third candidate is a doctor. He wants a new hospital.

Before the election, the candidates visit different parts of the town. They talk to people and listen to their ideas. Some people ask questions about roads, schools, and buses.

On election day, many people go to the town hall. They wait in line and vote for the person they like best.

In the evening, the votes are counted. Anna gets the most votes and becomes the new mayor.

Anna is happy, but she knows her work is not easy. The next morning, she meets other town workers. They talk about how to make the town better.

Anna says, “I want to listen to everyone and do my best.”

The people hope she can keep her promises.

## Questions

**Question 1: What does the town need?**

A. A new mayor.

B. A new school bus.

C. A new shop.

**Question 2: What is Anna's job before the election?**

A. She is a doctor.

B. She is a teacher.

C. She is a shop owner.

**Question 3: What does the shop owner want?**

A. More jobs for local people.

B. A bigger hospital.

C. A new train station.

**Question 4: What do people do on election day?**

A. They visit a hospital.

B. They go to school.

C. They vote.

**Question 5: Why does Anna become the new mayor?**

A. She is the oldest candidate.

B. She gets the most votes.

C. She owns a big shop.

**Question 6: What does Anna do the next morning?**

A. She meets other town workers.

B. She leaves the town.

C. She opens a new school.

**Question 7: What does Anna want to do as mayor?**

A. Listen to everyone and do her best.

B. Stop people from asking questions.

C. Close all the local shops.

## Answers

1. **A. A new mayor.** The town needs someone new to lead it.

2. **B. She is a teacher.** Anna works as a teacher before the election.

3. **A. More jobs for local people.** The shop owner wants to help people find work.

4. **C. They vote.** People go to the town hall to choose their new mayor.

5. **B. She gets the most votes.** The votes are counted, and Anna gets the highest number.

6. **A. She meets other town workers.** They talk about ways to make the town better.

7. **A. Listen to everyone and do her best.** Anna wants to hear people's ideas and work hard for the town.
