# A Short History of the United States

## Article

The history of the United States is long and interesting. Before Europeans arrived, many Native American peoples lived across the land. They had their own languages, cultures, towns, and ways of life.

In 1492, Christopher Columbus reached the Americas. After that, more Europeans came to North America. They built towns and colonies. Many people also came from Africa, but many were forced to come as enslaved people.

In 1776, thirteen British colonies declared their independence. They wanted to become a free country. July 4 became an important day in American history. Today, Americans celebrate it as Independence Day.

In 1787, the United States wrote its Constitution. It became the basic law of the country. George Washington became the first president in 1789.

In the 1800s, the country grew larger. But there was a serious problem: slavery. In 1861, a civil war began between the North and the South. The war ended in 1865. Slavery was ended by law, but equal rights were still a big challenge.

During the 1900s, the United States became a powerful country. It played an important role in two World Wars. In 1969, American astronauts walked on the Moon for the first time.

Today, the United States is known for its technology, movies, music, universities, and many different cultures. Its history includes great achievements, but also serious problems and difficult lessons.

## Questions

**Question 1: Who lived in North America before Europeans arrived?**

A. Only European people  
B. Native American peoples  
C. Only people from Africa  

**Question 2: In which year did thirteen colonies declare independence?**

A. 1492  
B. 1776  
C. 1865  

**Question 3: Who was the first president of the United States?**

A. George Washington  
B. Abraham Lincoln  
C. Christopher Columbus  

**Question 4: When did the American Civil War end?**

A. 1787  
B. 1861  
C. 1865  

**Question 5: What happened in 1969?**

A. The Constitution was written.  
B. American astronauts walked on the Moon.  
C. The Civil War began.  

**Question 6: Which statement about American history is correct?**

A. It includes achievements and serious problems.  
B. It has no difficult events.  
C. It began in 1969.  

## Answers

1. **B. Native American peoples.** Many different Native American peoples lived across the land before Europeans arrived.
2. **B. 1776.** The thirteen colonies declared their independence from Britain in 1776.
3. **A. George Washington.** He became the first president in 1789.
4. **C. 1865.** The Civil War ended in 1865, and slavery was abolished by law.
5. **B. American astronauts walked on the Moon.** The Apollo 11 mission reached the Moon in 1969.
6. **A. It includes achievements and serious problems.** American history has many important events and difficult lessons.
