
# Why Do Astronauts Float in Space?

## Article

Have you ever seen a video of astronauts floating inside a space station? They can move around without walking. But why does this happen?

On Earth, gravity pulls things toward the ground. When you jump, you come back down because of gravity.

In space, astronauts inside the International Space Station seem to float. The station is not far away from Earth. In fact, Earth's gravity is still strong there.

So why do astronauts float?

The space station and the astronauts are both moving around Earth very fast. They are falling toward Earth together, but they also move forward fast enough to keep going around the planet. This is called orbit.

Because the astronauts and the station fall together, astronauts feel weightless. They can float, push objects, and move through the station with their hands and feet.

Living in space is not easy. Astronauts must exercise every day because their muscles and bones can become weaker. They also need special food, clothes, and equipment.

Floating in space looks like fun, but it takes a lot of work to live there!

## Answers

1. **B. Gravity.** Gravity pulls objects toward the ground on Earth.
2. **C. On the International Space Station.** The astronauts live and work inside the station.
3. **A. They and the station fall around Earth together.** This makes them feel weightless.
4. **B. To move around a planet.** The space station travels around Earth in an orbit.
5. **B. To keep their muscles and bones strong.** Living in space can make muscles and bones weaker.
6. **B. No, astronauts need special food, clothes, and equipment.** Life in space requires special care and preparation.
