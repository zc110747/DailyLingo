# Getting to Know Microsoft

## Article

Microsoft is a famous technology company from the United States. It was started in 1975 by two young men, Bill Gates and Paul Allen. They wanted to make computer software easier for people to use.

In the early days, Microsoft made software for personal computers. Its first important product was a computer language called BASIC. Later, the company created Windows, an operating system for computers.

Windows became very popular around the world. It helps people open programs, save files, use the internet, and do many other things. Today, many offices, schools, and homes use Windows computers.

Microsoft also makes other popular products. Microsoft Word helps people write documents. Excel helps people work with numbers and tables. PowerPoint helps people make presentations. These programs are part of Microsoft 365.

In 2011, Microsoft bought Skype, a service for online calls. In 2016, it bought LinkedIn, a professional social network. It also owns Xbox, a video game brand.

Today, Microsoft works in many areas of technology. It provides cloud services through Azure and develops artificial intelligence tools, including Copilot. Its products help people study, work, communicate, and play games.

Microsoft has changed a lot since two young men started the company in 1975. It began with computer software and became one of the world's major technology companies.

4. **C. Excel.** Excel is used for numbers, tables, and calculations.
5. **A. A video game brand.** Xbox is Microsoft's gaming brand.
6. **A. Copilot.** Copilot is one of Microsoft's artificial intelligence tools.
