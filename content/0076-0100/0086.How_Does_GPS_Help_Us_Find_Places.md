# How Does GPS Help Us Find Places?

## Article

Have you ever used a phone to find a restaurant or a train station? Your phone may use GPS to show you the way.

GPS means Global Positioning System. It is a technology that helps people find their location on Earth.

How does it work? There are many GPS satellites moving around Earth. They send signals down to the ground. Your phone receives signals from several satellites and uses them to work out where you are.

After your phone finds your location, a map app can show your position on a map. You can choose a place and ask for directions.

GPS is useful in many ways. Drivers use it to find roads. Travelers use it to visit new places. Some people use it when they walk, run, or ride a bike.

GPS is also used by ships and airplanes. It helps them know where they are and plan their trips.

However, GPS is not perfect. Tall buildings can make signals weaker. In some places, such as tunnels, your phone may not find your location well.

Today, GPS is an important part of daily life. It helps people travel and find places more easily.

## Answers

1. **A. Their location.** GPS helps people know where they are.
2. **B. Global Positioning System.** This is the full name of GPS.
3. **C. Satellites.** GPS satellites send signals to devices on Earth.
4. **A. Your position on a map.** A map app can show where you are and help you find a route.
5. **B. The signals may be weak.** Tunnels and other places can make GPS signals difficult to receive.
6. **A. Ships and airplanes.** They can use GPS to know their positions and plan trips.
