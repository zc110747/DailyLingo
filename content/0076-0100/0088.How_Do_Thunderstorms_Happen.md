# How Do Thunderstorms Happen?

## Article

Have you ever heard a loud sound in the sky on a rainy day? That sound is thunder. It often comes with lightning and heavy rain.

A thunderstorm begins when warm, wet air rises into the sky. As the air goes higher, it becomes cooler. The water in the air turns into tiny drops and ice, and these form a large dark cloud.

Inside the cloud, tiny drops of water and pieces of ice move around. They hit each other and create electric charges. When the charges become strong enough, electricity moves through the air. We see this bright light as lightning.

Lightning heats the air very quickly. The hot air grows and moves very fast. This makes a loud sound called thunder.

Why do we see lightning before we hear thunder? Light travels much faster than sound. So we see the flash first and hear the sound a little later.

Thunderstorms can bring heavy rain, strong winds, and sometimes hail. During a storm, it is safer to stay inside a building. Do not stand under a tree or stay in an open field.

The next time you see lightning, remember: the bright flash and the loud sound are two parts of the same event!

## Questions

**Question 1: What is thunder?**

A. A loud sound in the sky

B. A kind of cloud

C. A strong wind

**Question 2: What happens when warm, wet air rises?**

A. It becomes hotter.

B. It becomes cooler.

C. It disappears.

**Question 3: What creates electric charges inside a storm cloud?**

A. The Sun and the Moon

B. Trees and buildings

C. Water drops and pieces of ice hitting each other

**Question 4: What causes the sound of thunder?**

A. The Moon moving

B. Lightning heating the air very quickly

C. Rain falling on the ground

**Question 5: Why do we see lightning before hearing thunder?**

A. Light travels faster than sound.

B. Thunder is always very quiet.

C. Lightning happens earlier than rain.

**Question 6: What is a safe place during a thunderstorm?**

A. Under a tall tree

B. In an open field

C. Inside a building

## Answers

1. **A. A loud sound in the sky.** Thunder is the sound we hear during a thunderstorm.
2. **B. It becomes cooler.** Air gets cooler as it rises higher.
3. **C. Water drops and pieces of ice hitting each other.** Their movement helps create electric charges inside the cloud.
4. **B. Lightning heating the air very quickly.** The rapidly heated air expands and creates the sound of thunder.
5. **A. Light travels faster than sound.** That is why the flash reaches our eyes before the sound reaches our ears.
6. **C. Inside a building.** Staying inside a building is safer during a thunderstorm.
