# A Robot in the Classroom

## Article

On Monday morning, a new robot comes to a school. The science teacher brings it into the classroom. The students are very excited.

The robot is small and white. It can move, speak, and answer simple questions. The teacher turns it on and asks, “What is your name?”

The robot says, “My name is Robo. Nice to meet you!”

The students laugh and clap their hands. Then a boy named Jack asks, “Can you help me with my homework?”

The robot says, “Yes, I can help you learn. But you must try the questions first.”

Jack thinks this is a good idea. He opens his book and starts to work. When he has a problem, the robot gives him a small hint. Jack finds the answer by himself.

Later, the teacher asks the students what they think about the robot. Some students want it to teach math. Others want it to tell stories or help them learn English.

The teacher smiles. She says, “A robot can be a useful tool, but people still need to think and learn.”

At the end of the day, Jack thanks the robot. He has learned something new, and he wants to build a robot one day.

## Answers

1. **A. The science teacher.** The teacher brings the new robot into the classroom on Monday.

2. **B. White.** The robot is small and white.

3. **C. Move, speak, and answer simple questions.** These are some of the robot's abilities.

4. **A. His homework.** Jack asks the robot to help him with his schoolwork.

5. **B. Try the questions himself.** The robot encourages Jack to think before asking for help.

6. **A. It gives him a small hint.** The hint helps Jack find the answer by himself.

7. **C. Teach math or tell stories.** The students have different ideas about how the robot can help them.

8. **A. Build a robot.** Jack enjoys learning with Robo and wants to make a robot in the future.
