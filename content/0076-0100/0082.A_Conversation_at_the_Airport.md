# A Conversation at the Airport

## Article

**Anna:** Excuse me. Is this the line for check-in?

**Man:** Yes, it is. Are you flying to London?

**Anna:** Yes. My flight leaves at ten thirty. What time is it now?

**Man:** It is nine fifteen. You have enough time.

**Anna:** Good. Where can I find the check-in desk?

**Man:** It is over there, next to the coffee shop.

**Anna:** Thank you. Do I need to show my passport?

**Man:** Yes. You need your passport and your ticket.

**Anna:** Here they are. Is this my boarding pass?

**Man:** Yes. Your gate is number twelve.

**Anna:** Where is gate twelve?

**Man:** Go straight and turn left. You will see the signs.

**Anna:** Great! Thank you for your help.

**Man:** You're welcome. Have a nice flight!

## Questions

A. At a hotel

B. At an airport

C. At a train station

**Question 2: Where is Anna flying?**

A. London

B. Paris

C. New York

**Question 3: What time does her flight leave?**

A. Nine fifteen

B. Ten o'clock

C. Ten thirty

**Question 4: What does Anna need to show?**

A. Her passport and ticket

B. Her school card

C. Her phone only

**Question 5: Which gate does Anna need to find?**

A. Gate ten

B. Gate twelve

C. Gate twenty

**Question 6: How can Anna find gate twelve?**

A. Go straight and turn left.

B. Go straight and turn right.

C. Go back to the coffee shop.

## Answers

1. **B. At an airport.** Anna is preparing to take a flight.
2. **A. London.** The man asks if she is flying to London, and she says yes.
3. **C. Ten thirty.** Anna says her flight leaves at ten thirty.
4. **A. Her passport and ticket.** The man tells her to show both documents.
5. **B. Gate twelve.** Her boarding pass shows gate number twelve.
6. **A. Go straight and turn left.** The man gives her these directions.
