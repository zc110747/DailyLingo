# Getting to Know Beef

## Article

Beef is meat from cows. People in many countries eat beef. It is a popular food in many different dishes.

There are many ways to cook beef. People can fry it, boil it, bake it, or cook it on a grill. Some people like beef soup, while others enjoy beef noodles or a beef sandwich.

Beef has protein, iron, and other nutrients. Protein helps our bodies grow and repair muscles. Iron helps our blood carry oxygen. However, eating too much red meat may not be good for our health. It is important to eat different kinds of food.

Different parts of a cow have different kinds of meat. Some parts are soft and good for steak. Other parts are harder and need more time to cook. A slow cooker can make tough beef soft and delicious.

Beef is also important in many food cultures. In the United States, people often enjoy hamburgers and steaks. In China, beef noodles are popular in many cities. In Japan, people enjoy dishes such as beef rice bowls.

Food brings people together. A family may cook a beef dish and enjoy it around the dinner table.
