# The Lion and the Mouse

## Article

One day, a big lion sleeps under a tree. A little mouse runs over his body. The lion wakes up and catches the mouse.

“Why are you on my body?” the lion asks.

“Please let me go,” says the mouse. “One day, I may help you.”

The lion laughs. “You are so small! How can you help me?” But he lets the mouse go.

A few days later, the lion walks into a hunter’s net. He cannot get out. He roars loudly. The little mouse hears him and runs to help.

The mouse bites the net with his sharp teeth. Soon, the net has a big hole, and the lion is free.

“Thank you,” says the lion. “I was wrong. Even a small animal can help a big one.”

The mouse smiles. They become good friends.

**Moral:** Do not judge others by their size. Everyone can help in some way.

## Questions

**Question 1: Where does the lion sleep?**

A. Under a tree  
B. In a house  
C. Near a river  

**Question 2: Why does the lion catch the mouse?**

A. The mouse takes his food.  
B. The mouse runs over his body.  
C. The mouse wakes him for dinner.  

**Question 3: What happens to the lion later?**

A. He falls into a river.  
B. He gets lost in a forest.  
C. He gets caught in a hunter’s net.  

**Question 4: How does the mouse help the lion?**

A. He bites the net.  
B. He calls a hunter.  
C. He brings some food.  

**Question 5: What does the lion learn?**

A. Small animals are always weak.  
B. Even a small animal can help.  
C. Mice cannot make friends.  

## Answers

1. **A. Under a tree.** The lion sleeps under a tree at the beginning.
2. **B. The mouse runs over his body.** This wakes the lion up.
3. **C. He gets caught in a hunter’s net.** The lion cannot escape by himself.
4. **A. He bites the net.** The mouse uses his sharp teeth to make a hole.
5. **B. Even a small animal can help.** The story teaches us not to judge others by their size.
