# Getting to Know Airplanes

## Article

An airplane is a machine that can fly through the sky. It carries people and goods from one place to another. Today, airplanes are an important part of modern life.

Most large airplanes have two wings, a long body, and a tail. They also have powerful engines. The wings help the airplane fly, and the engines help it move forward.

Before a flight, passengers go to the airport. They show their tickets, check their bags, and wait at the gate. When the airplane is ready, they get on board and find their seats.

During the flight, passengers can look out of the window. They may see clouds, mountains, or the sea. Some people sleep, read books, or watch movies. The flight may take one hour or many hours.

Airplanes make travel faster. A trip that takes many days by ship can take only a few hours by airplane. Pilots and other workers help make every flight safe.

## Questions

**Question 1: What can an airplane carry?**

A. Only food  
B. People and goods  
C. Only animals  

**Question 2: What helps an airplane fly?**

A. Its wings  
B. Its windows  
C. Its seats  

**Question 3: Where do passengers wait before a flight?**

A. At the gate  
B. At the farm  
C. At the library  

**Question 4: What can passengers see from an airplane window?**

A. Only roads  
B. Clouds, mountains, or the sea  
C. Only buildings  

**Question 5: Who flies an airplane?**

A. A pilot  
B. A teacher  
C. A farmer  

**Question 6: Why are airplanes important?**

A. They make travel faster.  
B. They make the sea smaller.  
C. They make people walk more.

## Answers

1. **B. People and goods.** Airplanes carry people and goods from one place to another.
2. **A. Its wings.** The wings help the airplane fly.
3. **A. At the gate.** Passengers wait at the gate before boarding.
4. **B. Clouds, mountains, or the sea.** These things can be seen from the window.
5. **A. A pilot.** A pilot flies the airplane.
6. **A. They make travel faster.** Airplanes help people travel long distances in less time.
