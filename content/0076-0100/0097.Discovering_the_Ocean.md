# Discovering the Ocean

## Article

The ocean is a very large body of salt water. It covers about 71 percent of the Earth's surface. There are five main oceans: the Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern, and Arctic Oceans.

The ocean is home to many kinds of animals. Fish, whales, dolphins, turtles, and sharks live in the water. Some animals are very small, while others are huge. The blue whale is the largest animal on Earth.

The ocean is also full of beautiful places. There are colorful coral reefs, dark deep-sea areas, and sandy beaches. Many fish live near coral reefs because they can find food and safe places there.

The ocean is important to people. It gives us food and helps move goods around the world. It also affects the weather and helps keep the Earth's temperature stable.

But the ocean has some problems. Plastic waste and dirty water can hurt sea animals. People can help by using less plastic, keeping beaches clean, and protecting sea life.

The ocean is still full of mysteries. Scientists explore deep water to learn about new animals and strange places. There is much more to discover!

## Questions

**Question 1: What kind of water is in the ocean?**

A. Fresh water  
B. Salt water  
C. Hot milk  

**Question 2: How many main oceans are there on Earth?**

A. Three  
B. Four  
C. Five  

**Question 3: What is the largest animal on Earth?**

A. The blue whale  
B. The dolphin  
C. The sea turtle  

**Question 4: Why do many fish live near coral reefs?**

A. They can find food and safe places.  
B. The water is always warm.  
C. There are no other animals there.  

**Question 5: What can hurt sea animals?**

A. Clean beaches  
B. Plastic waste  
C. Fresh air  

**Question 6: How can people help the ocean?**

A. Use more plastic.  
B. Throw rubbish into the sea.  
C. Keep beaches clean.  

## Answers

1. **B. Salt water.** Ocean water contains salt.
2. **C. Five.** The five main oceans cover different parts of the Earth.
3. **A. The blue whale.** It is the largest animal on Earth.
4. **A. They can find food and safe places.** Coral reefs provide homes for many sea animals.
5. **B. Plastic waste.** Plastic can hurt or trap sea animals.
6. **C. Keep beaches clean.** Clean beaches help protect ocean life.
