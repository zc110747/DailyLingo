# The Wrong Train

## Article

It is Friday afternoon. David is at the train station. He wants to visit his aunt in another town.

He looks at the train signs and finds his platform. Soon, a train arrives. David gets on and finds a seat near the window.

After ten minutes, he looks outside. The buildings look different from the ones he knows. He checks the train sign again and feels worried.

“Oh no! I am on the wrong train!” he says.

David gets off at the next station. He goes to the information desk and asks a worker for help.

“Excuse me. I want to go to Green Town. Which train should I take?”

The worker smiles and shows him the correct platform. David thanks the worker and waits for the next train.

This time, he checks the train number carefully before getting on.

An hour later, David arrives in Green Town. His aunt is waiting for him at the station.

“You are here!” she says happily.

David laughs. “Yes! I took the wrong train first, but I found the right one.”

A. He buys some food.

## Answers

1\. **A. Green Town.** David wants to visit his aunt in Green Town.

2\. **B. Near the window.** He finds a seat near the window after getting on the first train.

3\. **C. The buildings outside look different.** David notices that the buildings do not look familiar, so he checks the train sign.

4\. **A. An information desk worker.** The worker tells David which platform to use.

5\. **B. He checks the train number carefully.** David wants to make sure he gets on the correct train this time.
