# The Night of the Lanterns

## Article

Every year, a small town has a lantern festival. People come together in the evening to enjoy the lights, food, and music.

This year, Mia goes to the festival with her parents and her little brother, Tom.

When they arrive, the streets are full of colorful lanterns. Some lanterns look like flowers. Others look like fish, rabbits, and birds.

Tom sees a red lantern shaped like a dragon. He points at it and laughs.

“Look, Dad! The dragon is so big!”

His father smiles and takes a photo of him.

Mia wants to make her own lantern. At a small table, a kind woman shows her how to use paper, sticks, and glue. Mia chooses yellow and orange paper. She works carefully and makes a beautiful lantern.

Later, the family buys some warm food and sits near a small stage. A group of musicians plays traditional music. Many people clap their hands and smile.

Before they go home, Mia lights a small lantern with an electric light inside. She holds it up and looks at the bright colors around her.

“It is a wonderful night,” she says.

Her mother agrees. She tells the children that festivals help people share their culture and spend time together.

Mia and Tom walk home happily. They hope to come back next year.

## Questions

**Question 1: When does the lantern festival take place?**

A. In the morning.

B. In the afternoon.

C. In the evening.

**Question 2: Who goes to the festival with Mia?**

A. Her parents and her little brother.

B. Her teacher and classmates.

C. Her grandparents.

**Question 3: What does Tom see?**

A. A blue bird.

B. A red dragon lantern.

C. A yellow flower.

**Question 4: What materials does Mia use to make her lantern?**

A. Wood, paint, and cloth.

B. Glass, metal, and string.

C. Paper, sticks, and glue.

**Question 5: What does Mia's mother say about festivals?**

A. They help people share their culture and spend time together.

B. They are only for children.

C. They are too noisy.

## Answers

1. **C. In the evening.** The festival takes place in the evening, when people enjoy the lights and music.

2. **A. Her parents and her little brother.** Mia visits the festival with her family.

3. **B. A red dragon lantern.** Tom sees a large red lantern shaped like a dragon.

4. **C. Paper, sticks, and glue.** A woman shows Mia how to use these materials.

5. **A. They help people share their culture and spend time together.** Mia's mother explains why festivals are important to the community.
