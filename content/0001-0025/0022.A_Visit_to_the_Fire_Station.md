# A Visit to the Fire Station

## Article

On Friday morning, Lily's class visits a fire station. The children are very excited. They want to learn about the work of firefighters.

A firefighter named Mr. Brown welcomes them at the door. He shows them a big red fire truck. The truck has a long ladder, many hoses, and a lot of tools.

“This truck helps us put out fires,” Mr. Brown says.

Lily looks at the truck carefully. She asks, “Do you work every day?”

“Yes,” Mr. Brown answers. “We work day and night. We help people when they are in danger.”

Then the children learn what to do when they see a fire. Mr. Brown tells them to leave the building quickly and call for help. They must never hide or go back into a burning building.

Before the class leaves, the children take a photo with Mr. Brown beside the fire truck.

Lily is happy. She wants to tell her family everything she has learned.

## Answers

1\. **A. On Friday morning.** Lily's class visits the fire station on Friday morning.

2\. **B. Mr. Brown.** Mr. Brown welcomes the children and shows them the fire truck.

3\. **C. Red.** The fire truck is big and red.

4\. **B. Leave the building quickly and call for help.** Mr. Brown teaches the children how to stay safe during a fire.

5\. **A. They take a photo with Mr. Brown.** The children take a photo beside the fire truck before leaving.
