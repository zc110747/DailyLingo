# The School Art Show

## Article

This Friday, our school has an art show. Students put their pictures on the walls of the school hall.

Amy loves drawing animals. She brings a picture of a small bird. The bird is sitting on a tree, and the sun is shining in the sky.

Her friend Jack makes a model of a house. He uses paper, glue, and small pieces of wood. The house has a red roof and two blue windows.

Many parents come to see the students' work. They walk around the hall and look at the pictures and models.

A teacher gives a small prize to the best picture. Amy does not win the prize, but she is not sad. She is happy because many people like her bird.

At the end of the show, the students help clean the hall. Amy and Jack take their work home.

They have a good time, and they want to join the art show again next year.

## Questions

Question 1：When does the school have the art show?

A. This Monday.

B. This Wednesday.

C. This Friday.

Question 2：What does Amy draw?

A. A small bird.

B. A big house.

C. A beautiful flower.

Question 3：What does Jack use to make his model?

A. Paper, glue, and small pieces of wood.

B. Glass, metal, and paint.

C. Leaves, stones, and water.

Question 4：How does Amy feel when she does not win the prize?

A. She is angry.

B. She is happy because people like her picture.

C. She wants to leave the school.

Question 5：What do Amy and Jack do at the end of the show?

A. They go to the park.

B. They give their work to the teacher.

C. They help clean the hall and take their work home.

## Answers

1\. **C. This Friday.** The article says that the school has an art show this Friday.

2\. **A. A small bird.** Amy loves drawing animals, and she brings a picture of a small bird.

3\. **A. Paper, glue, and small pieces of wood.** Jack uses these materials to make his model house.

4\. **B. She is happy because people like her picture.** Amy does not win the prize, but she is pleased that many people like her bird.

5\. **C. They help clean the hall and take their work home.** At the end of the show, the students clean the hall, and Amy and Jack take their work home.
