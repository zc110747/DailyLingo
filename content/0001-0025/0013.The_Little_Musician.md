# The Little Musician

## Article

Mia is eight years old. She loves music, but she does not know how to play an instrument.

One day, her grandfather gives her a small guitar. It is brown and has six strings.

“This guitar is for you,” he says. “You can learn to play it.”

Mia is very happy. Every afternoon, she sits in her room and practices for twenty minutes. At first, the music sounds strange. Sometimes, her fingers hurt, and she wants to stop.

Her grandfather tells her to be patient. He shows her how to hold the guitar and play a few simple songs.

After two weeks, Mia can play a short song by herself. She plays it for her family after dinner.

Everyone smiles and claps. Mia feels proud of herself. She knows she still has a lot to learn, but she wants to keep playing music.

## Questions

Question 1：How old is Mia?

A. Six years old.

B. Eight years old.

C. Ten years old.

Question 2：What does Mia's grandfather give her?

A. A small guitar.

B. A new piano.

C. A music book.

Question 3：How long does Mia practice every afternoon?

A. Ten minutes.

B. Thirty minutes.

C. Twenty minutes.

Question 4：What happens after two weeks?

A. Mia buys another guitar.

B. Mia can play a short song.

C. Mia stops learning music.

Question 5：How does Mia feel when her family claps?

A. Proud and happy.

B. Angry and tired.

C. Sad and afraid.

## Answers

1\. **B. Eight years old.** The first sentence tells us that Mia is eight years old.

2\. **A. A small guitar.** Her grandfather gives her a brown guitar with six strings.

3\. **C. Twenty minutes.** Mia practices playing the guitar for twenty minutes every afternoon.

4\. **B. Mia can play a short song.** After two weeks of practice, she can play a short song by herself.

5\. **A. Proud and happy.** Her family smiles and claps, and Mia feels proud of what she has learned.
