# A Day at the Market

## Article

On Sunday morning, Lucy goes to the market with her mother. They need to buy some food for lunch.

The market is busy. Many people are buying fruit, vegetables, and meat. Lucy sees a man selling fresh apples.

“Can we buy some apples?” Lucy asks.

“Of course,” her mother says.

They buy six red apples and some bananas. Then they go to a vegetable shop. Lucy helps her mother choose some carrots and potatoes.

At the next shop, her mother buys some fish. Lucy carries a small bag, and her mother carries the other bags.

On the way home, Lucy sees a little dog near a tree. The dog looks hungry. Lucy gives it a small piece of bread.

When they get home, Lucy helps her mother wash the vegetables. She is happy because she helps her family today.

## Questions

Question 1：Who does Lucy go to the market with?

A. Her father.

B. Her mother.

C. Her sister.

Question 2：How many apples do they buy?

A. Four.

B. Five.

C. Six.

Question 3：What vegetables does Lucy help her mother choose?

A. Carrots and potatoes.

B. Tomatoes and onions.

C. Cabbages and beans.

Question 4：What does Lucy give the little dog?

A. A piece of bread.

B. An apple.

C. Some fish.

Question 5：What does Lucy do when she gets home?

A. She watches TV.

B. She washes the vegetables.

C. She goes to sleep.

## Answers

1\. **B. Her mother.** Lucy goes to the market with her mother on Sunday morning.

2\. **C. Six.** They buy six red apples and some bananas.

3\. **A. Carrots and potatoes.** Lucy helps her mother choose these vegetables at the vegetable shop.

4\. **A. A piece of bread.** Lucy sees a hungry dog near a tree and gives it a small piece of bread.

5\. **B. She washes the vegetables.** When they get home, Lucy helps her mother wash the vegetables.
