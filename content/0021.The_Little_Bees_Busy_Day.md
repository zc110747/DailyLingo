# The Little Bee’s Busy Day

## Article

It is a sunny morning. A little bee flies out of its home in a tree. It is ready to look for flowers.

The bee flies over a green field. It sees many yellow and pink flowers. It lands on a yellow flower and drinks a little sweet nectar.

Then the bee flies to another flower. It moves from flower to flower and collects nectar. The bee is small, but it works very hard.

A little boy named Sam sees the bee in the garden. He wants to look at it, but he does not touch it.

“Look, Mom! The bee is busy,” Sam says.

His mother smiles. “Yes. Bees help flowers grow by moving pollen from one flower to another.”

Sam watches the bee for a few minutes. Then he sees a butterfly near a red flower.

“What a beautiful day!” he says.

In the afternoon, the bee flies back to its home. It has had a busy day in the garden.

## Questions

**Question 1：Where does the bee live?**

A. In a tree.

B. In a house.

C. Under a table.

**Question 2：What color are some of the flowers?**

A. Black and white.

B. Yellow and pink.

C. Blue and gray.

**Question 3：What does the bee collect?**

A. Small stones.

B. Green leaves.

C. Nectar.

**Question 4：What does Sam do when he sees the bee?**

A. He touches the bee.

B. He watches the bee without touching it.

C. He takes the bee home.

**Question 5：How do bees help flowers?**

A. They move pollen from one flower to another.

B. They give flowers water.

C. They make flowers change color.

## Answers

1\. **A. In a tree.** The bee flies out of its home in a tree.

2\. **B. Yellow and pink.** The bee sees many yellow and pink flowers in the field.

3\. **C. Nectar.** The bee visits flowers and collects nectar.

4\. **B. He watches the bee without touching it.** Sam looks at the bee but does not touch it.

5\. **A. They move pollen from one flower to another.** This helps many plants grow and make seeds.
