# The World of War

## Article

War has changed the world many times throughout history. It can begin for different reasons, such as disagreements over land, power, money, or political ideas. Sometimes, countries try to solve their problems through talks, but these talks may fail.

During a war, soldiers often leave their homes and families to fight. Many people who are not soldiers also suffer. They may lose their homes, their jobs, or even their family members. Children may have to leave school and move to safer places.

World War I began in 1914 and ended in 1918. Many countries in Europe were involved, and millions of people died. World War II started in 1939 and ended in 1945. It was even larger and affected countries around the world. After these wars, many nations wanted to build a more peaceful future.

Today, countries use different ways to prevent war. They meet with other leaders, sign agreements, and work with international organizations. The United Nations was created in 1945 to help countries cooperate and maintain peace.

However, peace is not always easy to achieve. Countries may still have different interests and opinions. Learning about history helps people understand the terrible effects of war and the importance of solving problems peacefully.

Peace does not mean that everyone agrees. It means people are willing to listen, respect others, and search for solutions without violence.

A. Because everyone wants to travel.  
B. Because of disagreements over land, power, or ideas.  
C. Because countries have too many schools.

A. Only soldiers  
B. Only political leaders

**Question 3: When did World War I begin?**

A. 1918  
B. 1945  
C. 1950  

**Question 5: Why was the United Nations created?**

A. To help countries cooperate and maintain peace.  
B. To make countries fight more wars.  
C. To control every country's economy.  

**Question 6: What can countries do to prevent war?**

A. Stop talking to other countries.  
B. Meet with leaders and sign agreements.  
C. Close all their schools.  

**Question 7: What can people learn from history?**

A. War has no effects on ordinary people.  
B. Peace is always easy to achieve.  
C. War has terrible effects, and peaceful solutions are important.  

**Question 8: According to the article, what does peace mean?**

A. Everyone must have the same opinion.  
B. People listen, respect others, and solve problems without violence.  
C. Countries never discuss their problems.  

## Answers

1. **B. Because of disagreements over land, power, or ideas.** The article explains that conflicts can begin because of different interests.
2. **C. Soldiers and ordinary people.** People who are not soldiers may also lose homes, jobs, or family members.
3. **A. 1914.** World War I began in 1914 and ended in 1918.
4. **B. 1945.** World War II ended in 1945.
5. **A. To help countries cooperate and maintain peace.** The United Nations was established after World War II.
6. **B. Meet with leaders and sign agreements.** Talks and cooperation can help prevent conflict.
7. **C. War has terrible effects, and peaceful solutions are important.** History helps people understand the costs of war.
8. **B. People listen, respect others, and solve problems without violence.** Peace does not require everyone to agree.
