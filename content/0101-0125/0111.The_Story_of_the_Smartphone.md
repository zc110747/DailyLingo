# The Story of the Smartphone

## Article

The smartphone is one of the most useful objects in our daily lives. It is small enough to fit in a pocket, but it can do many things. People use smartphones to make phone calls, send messages, take photos, watch videos, and read books.

The first mobile phones were very different from modern smartphones. They were large, heavy, and expensive. People could only use them to make phone calls. Over time, mobile phones became smaller and lighter. In the early 2000s, more phones began to offer cameras, internet access, and other useful features.

Today, smartphones have powerful computers inside them. They have touch screens, cameras, microphones, and sensors. Some phones can recognize a person's face or fingerprint to unlock the screen. People can also use voice assistants to ask questions or control different functions.

Smartphones have changed how people communicate. We can talk to friends who live in other countries and share photos in seconds. Students can use them to study languages, watch educational videos, and search for information.

However, smartphones also have some disadvantages. Looking at a screen for too long may hurt our eyes. Some people spend too much time playing games or checking social media. It is important to use smartphones wisely and take breaks.

A smartphone is more than a phone. It is a camera, a computer, a map, and a library in one small device.

## Answers

1. **B. Make calls, take photos, and watch videos.** Smartphones have many different functions.
2. **A. Large, heavy, and expensive.** Early mobile phones were very different from today's devices.
3. **B. They became smaller and lighter.** Mobile phone technology improved over time.
4. **A. A person's face or fingerprint.** Some phones use these features for security.
5. **B. To study languages and find information.** Smartphones can support learning.
6. **C. Looking at screens for too long may hurt our eyes.** The article mentions this as a disadvantage.
7. **A. Use smartphones wisely and take breaks.** This helps people avoid spending too much time on screens.
8. **B. A camera, computer, map, and library.** A smartphone combines many useful tools in one device.
