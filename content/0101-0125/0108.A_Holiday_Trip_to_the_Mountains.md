# A Holiday Trip to the Mountains

## Article

Last summer, Emily and her family went on a holiday trip to the mountains. They wanted to escape the busy city and enjoy some fresh air. They stayed in a small hotel near a beautiful lake.

The journey took about four hours by car. Emily listened to music and looked out of the window. Along the way, she saw green forests, small villages, and high mountains. Her younger brother asked many questions about the places they passed.

On the first day, the family visited a local market. They bought fresh fruit, handmade gifts, and some snacks. In the afternoon, they walked around the lake. The water was clear, and they could see fish swimming near the shore. Emily took many photos of the mountains and flowers.

The next morning, they went hiking. At first, the path was easy, but it became steeper as they climbed higher. Their father walked slowly and reminded everyone to drink enough water. After two hours, they reached a viewpoint. The view was amazing. They could see the whole valley below them.

On the last day, the family visited a small museum and learned about the history of the mountain village. Before leaving, Emily bought a postcard for her best friend.

Although the trip was short, everyone had a wonderful time. Emily felt relaxed and happy. She hoped to visit the mountains again next year.

2. **C. About four hours.** The article says the journey took about four hours by car.
3. **A. She listened to music and looked out of the window.** She enjoyed the journey by listening to music and watching the scenery.
