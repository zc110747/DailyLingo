# Japan: A Country of Tradition and Technology

## Article

Japan is an island country in East Asia. It is located in the Pacific Ocean, close to China and South Korea. The country has thousands of islands, but four of them are the largest: Honshu, Hokkaido, Kyushu, and Shikoku. Tokyo, the capital city, is one of the largest cities in the world.

Japan has a long and interesting history. For hundreds of years, emperors were important symbols of the country. However, real political power was often held by military leaders called shoguns. During this period, samurai served powerful leaders and followed special rules about honor and loyalty.

In 1868, Japan began an important period of change called the Meiji Restoration. Before this time, Japan had limited contact with many foreign countries. The new government wanted to modernize the country. It introduced new schools, built railways, developed factories, and created a modern army. Japan learned from Western countries and became an important industrial power.

During the 20th century, Japan became involved in several wars. In 1941, Japan attacked the American naval base at Pearl Harbor in Hawaii. This brought the United States into World War II. In August 1945, after the atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki, Japan announced its surrender.

After the war, Japan faced many difficulties. Its cities and factories had suffered serious damage. However, the country worked hard to rebuild its economy. During the following decades, Japanese companies became famous around the world for making cars, cameras, televisions, and electronic products.

Today, Japan is known for both its traditional culture and modern technology. Visitors can see ancient temples, traditional gardens, and historic castles. At the same time, cities such as Tokyo and Osaka have modern buildings, fast trains, and advanced technology.

Japanese culture has also become popular internationally. Anime, manga, video games, and Japanese food attract people from many different countries.

Japan is an interesting example of how a country can protect its traditions while continuing to develop and change.

## Answers

1. **B. In East Asia.** Japan is an island country in East Asia, located in the Pacific Ocean.

2. **A. Tokyo.** Tokyo is the capital and one of the largest cities in the world.

3. **C. Military leaders called shoguns.** Although emperors were important, shoguns often controlled the government and military.

4. **B. The Meiji Restoration began.** This period brought major political, economic, and social changes to Japan.

5. **A. It built railways, developed factories, and introduced new schools.** These changes helped Japan develop modern industries and institutions.

6. **C. The United States entered World War II.** Japan's attack on Pearl Harbor on December 7, 1941, led the United States to enter the war.

7. **B. Cars, cameras, televisions, and electronic products.** Japanese companies became important manufacturers in the global market.

8. **A. Japan has developed while keeping many parts of its traditional culture.** Japan combines historical traditions with modern technology and international popular culture.
