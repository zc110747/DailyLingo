# The Yangtze River

## Article

The Yangtze River is the longest river in China and the third-longest river in the world. It is about 6,300 kilometers long. The river begins in the mountains of western China and flows east across the country before reaching the East China Sea near Shanghai.

The Yangtze River is very important to China. It provides water for millions of people and helps farmers grow crops. Many cities and towns have been built along the river. These places use the river for transportation, trade, and daily life.

One famous place along the Yangtze River is the Three Gorges. The area has high mountains, beautiful views, and deep valleys. Many tourists visit the region by boat. The Three Gorges Dam is also located on the river. It is one of the world's largest hydroelectric power stations and produces electricity for many people.

The river is home to many kinds of animals and plants. In the past, the Yangtze River was home to the Chinese river dolphin, also known as the baiji. Sadly, scientists believe that this animal is probably extinct because of human activities and changes to its environment.

Today, people are working to protect the river. They are trying to reduce pollution, protect wildlife, and improve the environment. In 2021, China introduced a ten-year fishing ban in important areas of the Yangtze River to help fish populations recover.

The Yangtze River is not only a natural wonder but also an important part of Chinese history and culture.
