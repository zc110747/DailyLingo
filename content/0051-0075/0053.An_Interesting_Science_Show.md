# An Interesting Science Show

## Article

Lucy loves science. On Friday afternoon, her school has a science show in the school hall.

A young scientist comes to the school. He brings a big box, some water, and a small machine. First, he puts water into the box. Then he turns on the machine. A small light shines inside the box.

“Light can travel through water,” he says. The students watch carefully. They are very interested.

Next, the scientist shows them a small robot. The robot can move forward, turn left, and stop. Lucy wants to know how it works.

“It follows simple instructions,” the scientist explains. “People write the instructions, and the robot follows them.”

After the show, Lucy talks to her science teacher. She wants to learn more about robots and machines. Her teacher gives her a book about simple science projects.

That evening, Lucy reads the book at home. She has many questions, and she wants to try a small project on the weekend.

## Questions

**Question 1: When does the school have the science show?**

A. On Friday afternoon.

B. On Saturday morning.

C. On Monday evening.

**Question 2: Where does the show take place?**

A. In the library.

B. In the school hall.

C. In the playground.

**Question 3: What can the small robot do?**

A. Cook food and wash clothes.

B. Sing many songs.

C. Move forward, turn left, and stop.

**Question 4: How does the robot work?**

A. It follows simple instructions.

B. It reads books by itself.

C. It drinks water.

**Question 5: What does Lucy do that evening?**

A. She goes to the cinema.

B. She reads a science book.

C. She visits the scientist.

## Answers

1. **A. On Friday afternoon.** The science show takes place on Friday afternoon.
2. **B. In the school hall.** The students watch the show in the school hall.
3. **C. Move forward, turn left, and stop.** These are the movements shown by the scientist.
4. **A. It follows simple instructions.** The scientist explains that people write instructions for the robot.
5. **B. She reads a science book.** Her teacher gives her a book, and Lucy reads it at home.
