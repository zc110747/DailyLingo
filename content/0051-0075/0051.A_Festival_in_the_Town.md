# A Festival in the Town

## Article

Every year, people in Green Town have a spring festival. It is a happy day for families and friends.

This year, the festival is on Saturday. The town square is full of people. Some people sell food, and others sell small gifts. There are flowers on the tables, and colorful flags hang above the street.

Anna goes to the festival with her parents. She buys a small cake and a red flower. Her father takes photos, and her mother listens to a local music group.

At noon, a woman tells a story about the town. She talks about old festivals and the people who lived there many years ago. Anna likes the story because it helps her learn about her town.

In the afternoon, Anna and her parents sit under a tree and eat lunch. They are tired but happy. Before they go home, Anna says, “Let’s come again next year!”

## Questions

**Question 1: When is the festival?**

A. On Monday.

B. On Saturday.

C. On Sunday.

**Question 2: Who goes to the festival with Anna?**

A. Her parents.

B. Her teacher.

C. Her friends.

**Question 3: What does Anna buy?**

A. A book and a hat.

B. A cake and a red flower.

C. Some toys and a bag.

**Question 4: What does the woman talk about?**

A. The town's old festivals.

B. Her new job.

C. A trip to another country.

**Question 5: How do Anna and her parents feel at the end of the day?**

A. Angry and sad.

B. Hungry and worried.

C. Tired but happy.

## Answers

1. **B. On Saturday.** The article says the festival is on Saturday.
2. **A. Her parents.** Anna visits the festival with her mother and father.
3. **B. A cake and a red flower.** She buys both things at the festival.
4. **A. The town's old festivals.** The woman tells people about festivals from the past.
5. **C. Tired but happy.** They sit down to eat lunch and enjoy the day together.
