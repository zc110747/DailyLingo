# The Turtle and the Rabbit

## Article

One sunny morning, a rabbit meets a turtle on a forest path.

The rabbit looks at the turtle and laughs. “You walk so slowly!” he says.

The turtle smiles. “Yes, I am slow, but I can keep going.”

The rabbit wants to have a race. The turtle agrees, and they start at a big tree. The rabbit runs very fast. Soon, he is far ahead of the turtle.

The rabbit looks back. He cannot see the turtle, so he sits under a tree to rest. The day is warm, and he soon falls asleep.

The turtle keeps walking. He does not stop. After some time, he passes the sleeping rabbit and reaches the finish line.

When the rabbit wakes up, he runs to the finish line. But it is too late. The turtle is already there.

The rabbit learns an important lesson: being fast is good, but being patient and working hard are important too.

## Questions

**Question 1: Why does the rabbit laugh at the turtle?**

A. The turtle walks slowly.

B. The turtle is sleeping.

C. The turtle cannot find the forest.

**Question 2: What does the rabbit do during the race?**

A. He goes home.

B. He sleeps under a tree.

C. He stops to eat lunch.

**Question 3: What lesson does the rabbit learn?**

A. It is always best to run fast.

B. It is important to be patient and keep going.

C. It is better not to have friends.

## Answers

1\. **A. The turtle walks slowly.** The rabbit laughs because the turtle cannot walk fast.

2\. **B. He sleeps under a tree.** The rabbit thinks he has plenty of time, so he stops to rest and falls asleep.

3\. **B. It is important to be patient and keep going.** The turtle wins because he continues walking while the rabbit sleeps.
