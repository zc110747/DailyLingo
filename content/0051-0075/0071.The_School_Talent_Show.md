# The School Talent Show

## Article

On Friday evening, Jack's school holds a talent show. Many students want to show their special skills.

Jack likes playing the guitar. He practices every day after school, but he feels nervous before the show. His best friend, Amy, tells him, “You can do it! Just enjoy the music.”

The school hall is full of students and parents. First, a girl sings a song. Then, two boys perform a funny dance. Everyone claps and smiles.

Finally, it is Jack's turn. He walks onto the stage and starts to play his guitar. At first, his hands feel cold. Then he sees his family in the front row, and he feels better.

When the music ends, the audience claps loudly. Jack is happy. He does not win a prize, but he is proud of himself.

After the show, Amy says, “You played very well!”

Jack smiles. He wants to play again next year.

## Questions

**Question 1: What does Jack like doing?**

A. Playing the guitar.

B. Cooking dinner.

C. Riding a horse.

**Question 2: Why does Jack feel better on the stage?**

A. He sees his family in the front row.

B. The show ends early.

C. He finds a new guitar.

**Question 3: How does Jack feel after the show?**

A. Angry because he loses his guitar.

B. Sad because nobody claps.

C. Happy and proud of himself.

## Answers

1\. **A. Playing the guitar.** Jack practices the guitar every day after school.

2\. **A. He sees his family in the front row.** Seeing his family helps Jack feel less nervous.

3\. **C. Happy and proud of himself.** The audience enjoys his music, and Jack is proud even though he does not win a prize.
