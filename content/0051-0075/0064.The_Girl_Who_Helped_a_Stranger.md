# The Girl Who Helped a Stranger

## Article

One afternoon, Sophie walks home from school. Near the bus stop, she sees an old woman with two heavy bags.

The woman looks tired. She tries to lift one bag, but it is too heavy for her.

Sophie walks over and asks, “Can I help you?”

The woman smiles and says, “Yes, please. Thank you!”

Sophie carries one bag to the next street. The woman walks slowly beside her. They talk about the weather and the flowers in the gardens.

Soon, they reach the woman's house. She thanks Sophie and offers her a glass of water. Sophie drinks the water and says goodbye.

When Sophie gets home, she tells her mother about the woman. Her mother smiles and says, “A small act of kindness can make someone's day better.”

Sophie feels happy. She knows that helping others does not always take a lot of time.

## Questions

**Question 1: Where does Sophie see the old woman?**

A. Near the bus stop.

B. Inside a school.

C. At the supermarket.

**Question 2: What does Sophie do to help the woman?**

A. She buys her a new bag.

B. She carries one of her bags.

C. She calls a taxi.

**Question 3: What does Sophie's mother say?**

A. Children should never talk to anyone.

B. Helping others is always difficult.

C. A small act of kindness can make someone's day better.

## Answers

1\. **A. Near the bus stop.** Sophie sees the old woman with two heavy bags near the bus stop.

2\. **B. She carries one of her bags.** Sophie helps the woman carry a bag to her house.

3\. **C. A small act of kindness can make someone's day better.** Sophie's mother explains that even a small act can help someone feel better.
