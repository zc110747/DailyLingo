# A Day at the Space Center

## Article

On Saturday, Oliver visits a space center with his sister. He loves learning about planets and stars.

At the entrance, they see a large model of a rocket. A guide tells them that rockets can carry people and machines into space.

Inside, Oliver tries a space game. He sits in a small chair and looks at a screen. The game shows him how an astronaut moves around a space station.

His sister enjoys a room with pictures of the Moon. She learns that astronauts first walked on the Moon in 1969.

After lunch, they watch a short film about life in space. The film shows astronauts eating, sleeping, and working without normal gravity.

Before they leave, Oliver buys a picture of the Earth from the gift shop. He puts it on his bedroom wall.

Oliver feels excited. He wants to learn more about space and how rockets work.

## Questions

**Question 1: What does Oliver see at the entrance?**

A. A large model of a rocket.

B. A real spaceship.

C. A swimming pool.

**Question 2: In which year did astronauts first walk on the Moon?**

A. 1959.

B. 1969.

C. 1979.

**Question 3: What does Oliver buy before leaving?**

A. A toy car.

B. A book about cooking.

C. A picture of the Earth.

## Answers

1\. **A. A large model of a rocket.** Oliver and his sister see the rocket model when they enter the space center.

2\. **B. 1969.** The first people to walk on the Moon did so in 1969.

3\. **C. A picture of the Earth.** Oliver buys the picture from the gift shop and puts it on his bedroom wall.
