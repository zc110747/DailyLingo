# The Little Food Truck

## Article

Every morning, a small food truck parks near the city train station. It belongs to Mr. Chen, who sells sandwiches and fresh juice.

Many people stop at the truck before going to work. Some buy eggs and cheese sandwiches. Others choose apple juice or orange juice.

One morning, a woman named Kate arrives in a hurry. She has an important meeting at nine o'clock. She wants to buy breakfast, but there are many people waiting.

Mr. Chen sees that Kate is in a hurry. He prepares her sandwich quickly and gives her a bottle of water.

“Thank you for your help,” Kate says.

“You're welcome,” Mr. Chen replies with a smile.

Kate arrives at work on time. At lunch, she tells her friends about the kind man at the food truck. The next morning, two of her friends visit the truck, too.

Mr. Chen is happy to meet new customers. He believes that good food and friendly service can make people smile.

## Questions

**Question 1: Where does Mr. Chen park his food truck?**

A. Near the city train station.

B. Outside a hospital.

C. In front of a school.

**Question 2: Why is Kate in a hurry?**

A. She wants to catch a plane.

B. She has an important meeting.

C. She needs to visit her friend.

**Question 3: What happens the next morning?**

A. Mr. Chen closes his truck.

B. Kate buys a new car.

C. Two of Kate's friends visit the food truck.

## Answers

1\. **A. Near the city train station.** Mr. Chen parks his food truck near the station every morning.

2\. **B. She has an important meeting.** Kate needs to arrive at work on time for her meeting.

3\. **C. Two of Kate's friends visit the food truck.** Kate tells her friends about Mr. Chen's friendly service, so they come to try his food.
