# How Bees Help Flowers

## Article

Bees are small insects, but they do an important job. They help many flowers and plants grow.

On a warm spring day, a bee flies from flower to flower. It looks for sweet liquid called nectar. The bee drinks the nectar and collects pollen on its body.

When the bee visits another flower, some pollen moves from its body to the flower. This helps the plant make seeds. Later, some plants grow fruit, such as apples and pears.

Bees also make honey. They take nectar back to their home, called a hive. There, they make food for the bee family.

Tom likes watching bees in his garden. He does not touch them because he knows they can sting. Instead, he plants more flowers to give them food.

Tom is happy when he sees bees in his garden. He knows that even small animals can help nature.

## Questions

**Question 1: What does a bee look for in a flower?**

A. Nectar.

B. Water bottles.

C. Small stones.

**Question 2: How do bees help plants?**

A. They carry leaves to other plants.

B. They move pollen between flowers.

C. They make the ground dry.

**Question 3: Why does Tom plant more flowers?**

A. He wants to sell them.

B. He wants to keep bees out of his garden.

C. He wants to give bees more food.

## Answers

1\. **A. Nectar.** Bees visit flowers to find and drink nectar.

2\. **B. They move pollen between flowers.** When bees visit different flowers, they help move pollen and allow many plants to make seeds.

3\. **C. He wants to give bees more food.** Tom plants flowers because they provide food for bees.
