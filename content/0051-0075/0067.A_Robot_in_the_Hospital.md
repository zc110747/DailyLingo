# A Robot in the Hospital

## Article

A new robot works in a hospital. Its name is Robin. It is small and has two wheels. It can move through the halls and carry small boxes.

Every morning, Robin helps the nurses. It takes medicine and clean towels to different rooms. The nurses can spend more time caring for patients because Robin does some simple jobs.

One day, a little boy sees Robin near his room. He asks, “Can you play with me?”

Robin cannot play games, but it can make a funny sound. The boy laughs, and his mother smiles.

A nurse tells the boy that Robin is a helpful machine. It cannot replace doctors or nurses, but it can make their work easier.

The boy waves goodbye when Robin leaves the room. He hopes to see it again tomorrow.

## Questions

**Question 1: What can Robin do?**

A. Drive an ambulance.

B. Carry small boxes.

C. Perform an operation.

**Question 2: How does Robin help the nurses?**

A. It brings medicine and clean towels to rooms.

B. It teaches the nurses to dance.

C. It cooks every meal for the hospital.

**Question 3: What does the nurse say about Robin?**

A. It can replace all doctors.

B. It can treat every illness.

C. It can make the nurses' work easier.

## Answers

1\. **B. Carry small boxes.** Robin moves through the hospital and carries small boxes.

2\. **A. It brings medicine and clean towels to rooms.** Robin does simple jobs so the nurses have more time to care for patients.

3\. **C. It can make the nurses' work easier.** Robin helps with some tasks, but it cannot replace doctors or nurses.
