# The Old Bridge

## Article

There is an old bridge over the river near Hill Town. It was built many years ago. In the past, people used it to walk from one side of the river to the other.

Today, many cars use a new bridge nearby. The old bridge is only for people who walk or ride bicycles.

One Saturday, Ben visits the bridge with his grandfather. His grandfather tells him stories about the town. When he was a boy, there was no new bridge. People walked across the old one to go to school and buy food.

Ben looks at the stones and the wooden sides. He takes a photo of the river below.

“Why don't we pull down the old bridge?” Ben asks.

His grandfather explains that the bridge is part of the town's history. People want to keep it because it reminds them of life in the past.

Ben understands. He thinks old places can teach people many things.

## Questions

**Question 1: Who visits the old bridge with Ben?**

A. His grandfather.

B. His teacher.

C. His brother.

**Question 2: What do people use the old bridge for today?**

A. Driving large trucks.

B. Walking or riding bicycles.

C. Landing airplanes.

**Question 3: Why do people want to keep the old bridge?**

A. It is the newest bridge in town.

B. It is the only place to buy food.

C. It reminds them of the town's history.

## Answers

1\. **A. His grandfather.** Ben visits the old bridge with his grandfather on Saturday.

2\. **B. Walking or riding bicycles.** Cars use the new bridge, while the old bridge is for people on foot or bicycles.

3\. **C. It reminds them of the town's history.** The bridge helps people remember what life was like in the past.
