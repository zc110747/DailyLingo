# The Town's First Solar Bus

## Article

A town has a new bus. It uses energy from the sun to help charge its battery. The bus is quiet, and it does not make smoke from an exhaust pipe while it travels.

On Monday morning, Mr. Green drives the bus for the first time. Many people wait at the bus stop. They want to try the new bus.

A little girl sits near the window with her mother. She looks outside and sees cars, shops, and trees. She says, “This bus is so quiet!”

The driver smiles. He explains that the bus uses electricity to move. Solar panels can help provide some of the energy used to charge its battery.

The bus takes people to school, work, and the market. At the end of the day, Mr. Green parks it at the bus station.

The town hopes to use more clean buses in the future. People are happy to see a new way to travel.

## Questions

**Question 1: What helps charge the bus's battery?**

A. Energy from the sun can help.

B. Water from the river.

C. Wind from a fan inside the bus.

**Question 2: How does the girl describe the bus?**

A. It is too slow.

B. It is very noisy.

C. It is very quiet.

**Question 3: Where does the bus take people?**

A. Only to the airport.

B. To school, work, and the market.

C. Only to the beach.

## Answers

1\. **A. Energy from the sun can help.** Solar panels can help provide energy to charge the bus's battery.

2\. **C. It is very quiet.** The girl notices that the bus makes little noise.

3\. **B. To school, work, and the market.** The bus helps people travel to different places around town.
