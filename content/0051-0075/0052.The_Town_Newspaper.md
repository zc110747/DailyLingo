# The Town Newspaper

## Article

Ben is a young boy. He likes reading the town newspaper with his grandfather every Sunday.

One morning, Ben sees an interesting story on the front page. A small school garden has a lot of new vegetables. The students grow tomatoes, carrots, and beans. They work in the garden after class.

Ben wants to see the garden. He and his grandfather walk to the school. A teacher welcomes them and shows them the plants. Some students water the vegetables, and others take out small weeds.

“We want to give some vegetables to older people in our town,” says a student. “We are happy to help them.”

Ben thinks this is a great idea. He wants to start a small garden at home, too. His grandfather smiles and says, “We can plant some tomatoes next weekend.”

Ben is excited. He knows that a small garden can do something good for other people.

## Questions

**Question 1: When does Ben read the newspaper with his grandfather?**

A. Every Monday.

B. Every Sunday.

C. Every Friday.

**Question 2: What does Ben read about?**

A. A school garden.

B. A new train station.

C. A football game.

**Question 3: What vegetables do the students grow?**

A. Potatoes, onions, and corn.

B. Apples, pears, and oranges.

C. Tomatoes, carrots, and beans.

**Question 4: Who will receive some vegetables?**

A. Older people in the town.

B. The school teachers only.

C. People in another country.

**Question 5: What does Ben want to do?**

A. Write a newspaper.

B. Start a small garden at home.

C. Open a vegetable shop.

## Answers

1. **B. Every Sunday.** Ben reads the newspaper with his grandfather on Sundays.
2. **A. A school garden.** The story on the front page is about a garden at a local school.
3. **C. Tomatoes, carrots, and beans.** These are the three vegetables named in the article.
4. **A. Older people in the town.** The students want to share their vegetables with them.
5. **B. Start a small garden at home.** Ben likes the idea and wants to grow vegetables, too.
