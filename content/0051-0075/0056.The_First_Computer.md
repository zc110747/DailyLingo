# The First Computer

## Article

Many years ago, computers were very different from the computers we use today.

The first computers were very large. Some of them filled a whole room. They were expensive, and only a few people could use them.

People used these computers to do difficult math and study important problems. They did not have small screens or mice like many computers today.

Now, computers are much smaller. People use them at home, at school, and at work. They can write letters, watch videos, learn new things, and talk to friends.

Lucy is a student. She uses a computer to learn English. She listens to stories and writes new words in a notebook.

She thinks computers are useful. She also knows that books, teachers, and real-life practice are important.

## Questions

**Question 1: What were some early computers like?**

A. They were very large.

B. They were very small.

C. They were easy to carry.

**Question 2: What did people use early computers for?**

A. Making bread.

B. Doing difficult math.

C. Taking photos of birds.

**Question 3: How does Lucy use a computer?**

A. She uses it to drive a car.

B. She uses it to cook dinner.

C. She uses it to learn English.

## Answers

1\. **A. They were very large.** Some early computers filled a whole room.

2\. **B. Doing difficult math.** People used early computers to do calculations and study important problems.

3\. **C. She uses it to learn English.** Lucy listens to stories and writes new words in her notebook.
