# The Little Town Newspaper

## Article

Every Friday, Lily helps her father at the town newspaper office. Her father is a reporter. He writes stories about people and events in the town.

This week, Lily has a special job. She talks to people at the local park. She asks them one question: “What do you like about our town?”

An old man likes the quiet streets. A young woman likes the new library. A boy likes the football field. Lily writes their answers in her notebook.

In the afternoon, she gives her notes to her father. He helps her write a short story for the newspaper. They add a photo of the park.

On Saturday morning, Lily sees the newspaper in a shop. Her story is on the front page. She feels proud and shows it to her friends.

Lily learns that a good news story can help people learn more about their town.

## Questions

**Question 1: What does Lily's father do?**

A. He is a reporter.

B. He is a doctor.

C. He is a shop owner.

**Question 2: What question does Lily ask people?**

A. What is your favorite food?

B. What do you like about our town?

C. Where do you work every day?

**Question 3: Where does Lily see her story?**

A. On a school wall.

B. In a library book.

C. On the front page of a newspaper.

## Answers

1\. **A. He is a reporter.** Lily's father writes stories about people and events in the town.

2\. **B. What do you like about our town?** Lily asks local people what they enjoy about their town.

3\. **C. On the front page of a newspaper.** Lily sees her story in the newspaper on Saturday morning.
