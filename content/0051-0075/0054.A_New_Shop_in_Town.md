# A New Shop in Town

## Article

A new shop opens on Main Street. It sells clothes for men, women, and children. The shop is small, but it has many beautiful things.

On Saturday morning, Emma visits the shop with her sister. They want to buy new clothes for a family dinner.

A friendly shop worker says hello to them. He shows Emma a blue dress and a white shirt. Emma likes the blue dress, but it is too big. She tries a smaller one, and it fits well.

Her sister finds a yellow shirt. She looks at the price and smiles. The shirt is not expensive, so she buys it.

At the door, they see a sign. It says, “Buy two things and get one small gift!”

Emma buys a pair of socks, too. The shop worker gives her a little bag as a gift. Emma and her sister leave the shop with big smiles.

They are ready for their family dinner.

## Questions

**Question 1: What does the new shop sell?**

A. Food and drinks.

B. Clothes.

C. Books and toys.

**Question 2: Why do Emma and her sister buy clothes?**

A. They need clothes for a family dinner.

B. They want to go swimming.

C. They are going to school.

**Question 3: Why does Emma try a smaller dress?**

A. The blue dress is too big.

B. The blue dress is too short.

C. The blue dress is too expensive.

**Question 4: What does Emma's sister buy?**

A. A blue dress.

B. A white shirt.

C. A yellow shirt.

**Question 5: What gift does Emma receive?**

A. A little bag.

B. A new dress.

C. A pair of shoes.

## Answers

1. **B. Clothes.** The new shop sells clothes for men, women, and children.
2. **A. They need clothes for a family dinner.** They visit the shop to find clothes for the dinner.
3. **A. The blue dress is too big.** Emma tries a smaller dress, and it fits well.
4. **C. A yellow shirt.** Her sister likes the shirt because it is not expensive.
5. **A. A little bag.** Emma receives a little bag because she buys two things.
