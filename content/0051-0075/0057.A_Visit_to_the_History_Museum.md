# A Visit to the History Museum

## Article

On Saturday, Jack visits the history museum with his class. Their teacher, Miss Green, meets them at the front door.

Inside the museum, they see old clothes, pictures, and tools. A guide tells them about life in the past. Many years ago, people did not have cars or computers. They walked to work and wrote letters by hand.

Jack stops in front of an old map. It shows his town a hundred years ago. There are only a few houses near the river. Today, there are many shops, schools, and tall buildings.

After the visit, Jack buys a small book about the town. He wants to show it to his grandfather.

On the way home, Jack says, “History is interesting. It helps us understand how people lived before us.”

## Questions

**Question 1: Who visits the museum with Jack?**

A. His football coach.

B. His class.

C. His neighbors.

**Question 2: What does the old map show?**

A. Jack's town a hundred years ago.

B. A new shopping center.

C. A road to another country.

**Question 3: Why does Jack buy a small book?**

A. He wants to give it to his teacher.

B. He wants to use it to learn math.

C. He wants to show it to his grandfather.

## Answers

1\. **B. His class.** Jack visits the history museum with his classmates and teacher.

2\. **A. Jack's town a hundred years ago.** The map shows that the town had only a few houses near the river.

3\. **C. He wants to show it to his grandfather.** Jack buys a book about the town to share with his grandfather.
