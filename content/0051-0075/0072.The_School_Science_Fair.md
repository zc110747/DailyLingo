# The School Science Fair

## Article

Mia is a student at Green School. She likes science and wants to learn new things.

One Monday, her teacher tells the class about a science fair. Every student can make something and show it to other people.

Mia has an idea. She wants to make a small boat with paper and wood. She thinks the boat can move on water.

After school, Mia works on her boat with her father. They use paper, small pieces of wood, and glue. On Friday, the boat is ready.

At the science fair, Mia puts her boat in a large bowl of water. The boat floats! Her classmates are happy and ask her many questions.

Mia is proud of her work. She learns that science can be fun, and small ideas can become great projects.

## Questions

**Question 1: What does Mia want to make?**

A. A paper plane

B. A small boat

C. A toy car

**Question 2: Who helps Mia after school?**

A. Her father

B. Her teacher

C. Her friend

**Question 3: What happens to the boat at the science fair?**

A. It sinks.

B. It breaks.

C. It floats.

**Question 4: How does Mia feel about her work?**

A. Proud

B. Angry

C. Tired

**Question 5: What does Mia learn?**

A. Science is always difficult.

B. Science can be fun.

C. Boats must be made of metal.

## Answers

1. **B. A small boat.** Mia wants to make a small boat that can move on water.
2. **A. Her father.** Her father helps her make the boat after school.
3. **C. It floats.** The boat stays on the water in a large bowl.
4. **A. Proud.** Mia is proud of the boat she makes.
5. **B. Science can be fun.** Mia enjoys making and testing her boat.
