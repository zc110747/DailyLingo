# A New Bike Shop

## Article

A new bike shop opens on Main Street. It is called Happy Wheels. The shop sells bikes for children and adults.

On Saturday, Ben visits the shop with his father. Ben wants a new bike for his birthday. He sees a red bike, a blue bike, and a green bike.

The shop owner, Mr. White, helps Ben choose a bike. Ben tries the blue bike outside the shop. It is light and easy to ride.

His father asks the price. The bike costs one hundred dollars. His father thinks it is a good price, so he buys it for Ben.

Ben is very happy. He wears his helmet and rides his new bike in the park. He thanks his father and promises to ride carefully.

## Questions

**Question 1: What is the new shop called?**

A. Happy Wheels.

B. Blue Bikes.

C. Main Street Store.

**Question 2: Which bike does Ben choose?**

A. The red bike.

B. The blue bike.

C. The green bike.

**Question 3: How much does the bike cost?**

A. Fifty dollars.

B. Eighty dollars.

C. One hundred dollars.

## Answers

1\. **A. Happy Wheels.** The new bike shop on Main Street is called Happy Wheels.

2\. **B. The blue bike.** Ben tries the blue bike and finds it light and easy to ride.

3\. **C. One hundred dollars.** Ben's father asks the price and buys the bike for him.
