# The Girl Who Loved the Stars

## Article

Every night, Nora looks at the sky from her bedroom window. She likes seeing the stars and the moon.

One evening, her science teacher tells the class about a special event. There will be a meteor shower that night. Many small pieces of rock will move through the sky and make bright lines of light.

Nora is excited. After dinner, she goes outside with her father. They find a dark place away from the town lights. Her father brings two chairs and a warm blanket.

At first, Nora sees only a few stars. Then she sees a bright line move across the sky. Soon, another one appears.

“How beautiful!” she says.

Her father explains that people have watched the night sky for thousands of years. Stars helped travelers find their way before people had modern tools.

Nora wants to learn more about space. She decides to borrow a book about the stars from the library.

## Questions

**Question 1: What special event does Nora's teacher talk about?**

A. A music show.

B. A meteor shower.

C. A football game.

**Question 2: Where do Nora and her father watch the sky?**

A. In a dark place away from the town lights.

B. Inside a busy shopping center.

C. On a school bus.

**Question 3: What does Nora want to borrow from the library?**

A. A book about cooking.

B. A book about sports.

C. A book about the stars.

## Answers

1\. **B. A meteor shower.** Nora's science teacher tells the class that a meteor shower will happen that night.

2\. **A. In a dark place away from the town lights.** They choose a dark place so they can see the sky more clearly.

3\. **C. A book about the stars.** Nora wants to learn more about space after watching the meteor shower.
