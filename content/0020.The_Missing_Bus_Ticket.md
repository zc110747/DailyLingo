# The Missing Bus Ticket

## Article

It is Monday morning. Anna is going to the city by bus. She gets up early and has breakfast with her brother.

At the bus stop, Anna looks in her bag. She wants to find her bus ticket, but it is not there.

“Oh no! Where is my ticket?” she says.

She looks in her pockets and checks her schoolbag again. She cannot find it. The bus is coming, and Anna feels worried.

Her brother asks, “Did you put it on the kitchen table?”

Anna thinks for a moment. Then she remembers her breakfast. She puts her bag on the kitchen table when she eats.

“I think you are right!” Anna says.

Her brother calls their mother. Their mother finds the ticket on the table and takes a photo of it. Anna uses her phone to buy a new ticket online, so she can get on the bus.

On the bus, Anna thanks her brother. She decides to put her ticket in the same pocket every time.

B. Put her ticket in the same pocket.

## Answers

1\. **B. To the city.** Anna is going to the city by bus.

2\. **A. Her bus ticket.** She cannot find her bus ticket in her bag.

3\. **C. On the kitchen table.** Her brother remembers that Anna put her bag on the kitchen table during breakfast.

4\. **A. She uses her phone.** Anna buys a new ticket online with her phone.

5\. **B. Put her ticket in the same pocket.** She wants to keep her ticket in one place so she can find it easily.
