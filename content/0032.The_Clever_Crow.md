# The Clever Crow

## Article

It is a hot day in summer. A crow flies over a small village. It is very thirsty and wants to find some water.

The crow looks around. It sees a garden, a house, and a small road. Then it finds a bottle near a tree.

The crow looks inside the bottle. There is a little water at the bottom, but its beak cannot reach it.

The crow thinks for a moment. Then it sees some small stones on the ground.

The crow picks up a stone with its beak and drops it into the bottle. Then it picks up another stone. It does this again and again.

Slowly, the water rises in the bottle. At last, the crow can reach the water and drink it.

The crow feels happy and flies away.

A young girl watches the crow from her window. She tells her father what she sees.

“Animals can be very clever,” her father says.

The girl smiles. She learns that a small idea can solve a big problem.

## Questions

**Question 1: Why does the crow look for water?**

A. It is hungry.

B. It is thirsty.

C. It wants to swim.

**Question 2: Where does the crow find the bottle?**

A. Near a tree.

B. In a shop.

C. Inside a house.

**Question 3: Why can the crow not drink the water at first?**

A. The water is too cold.

B. The bottle is too heavy.

C. Its beak cannot reach the water.

**Question 4: What does the crow put into the bottle?**

A. Leaves.

B. Small stones.

C. Flowers.

**Question 5: What does the girl learn from the crow?**

A. Small ideas can solve big problems.

B. Birds should live in houses.

C. Water is not important.

## Answers

1. **B. It is thirsty.** The crow is hot and wants to find some water.

2. **A. Near a tree.** The crow finds a bottle near a tree.

3. **C. Its beak cannot reach the water.** There is only a little water at the bottom of the bottle.

4. **B. Small stones.** The crow drops stones into the bottle to make the water rise.

5. **A. Small ideas can solve big problems.** The crow finds a clever way to reach the water.
