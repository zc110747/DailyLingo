# An Ancient City

## Article

Many years ago, people built a beautiful city near a river. The city had high walls, large gates, and many small houses.

People went to the market every morning. They bought rice, vegetables, fruit, and meat. Some people made clothes, and others made tools. Traders came from far away to sell their goods.

The river was very important to the city. People used its water for drinking, cooking, and washing. They also used boats to carry food and other things.

Today, the old city is no longer full of people. Some houses are gone, but parts of the walls are still there. Visitors come to see the old buildings and learn about life in the past.

A guide tells visitors stories about the people who lived there. Children look at old bowls, coins, and tools in a small museum.

The city teaches us something important. People lived differently in the past, but they also worked, traded, and cared for their families.

History helps us understand how people lived long ago.

## Questions

**Question 1：Where did people build the ancient city?**

A. Near a river.

B. On a high mountain.

C. In a large forest.

**Question 2：What did people buy at the market?**

A. Computers and phones.

B. Rice, vegetables, fruit, and meat.

C. Cars and buses.

**Question 3：Why was the river important?**

A. People used it only for games.

B. People used it to build high walls.

C. People used its water and boats.

**Question 4：What can visitors see in the museum?**

A. Old bowls, coins, and tools.

B. New televisions and computers.

C. Modern cars and trains.

**Question 5：What can history help us understand?**

A. How to make a new phone.

B. How people lived long ago.

C. How to travel to the moon.

## Answers

1\. **A. Near a river.** The ancient city was built near a river.

2\. **B. Rice, vegetables, fruit, and meat.** People went to the market to buy food and other things.

3\. **C. People used its water and boats.** The river provided water for daily life, and boats helped carry goods.

4\. **A. Old bowls, coins, and tools.** These objects help visitors learn about life in the past.

5\. **B. How people lived long ago.** History helps us understand the lives and activities of people in earlier times.
