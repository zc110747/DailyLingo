# The Blue Planet

## Article

Earth is our home. It is a beautiful planet in space.

From space, Earth looks blue and green. The blue parts are oceans and seas. The green and brown parts are land.

There are many animals and plants on Earth. People live in cities, towns, and villages. Some people live near the sea, and others live near mountains.

Water is very important for life. People need water to drink, cook, and wash. Plants need water to grow, too.

The Earth is our home, and we need to take care of it. We can save water, plant trees, and keep our streets clean.

Even small actions can help make our planet a better place.

## Questions

Question 1：What color does Earth look from space?

A. Blue and green.

B. Red and yellow.

C. Black and white.

Question 2：What are the blue parts of Earth?

A. Mountains and forests.

B. Oceans and seas.

C. Cities and towns.

Question 3：Why do people need water?

A. To fly into space.

B. To make mountains.

C. To drink, cook, and wash.

Question 4：Which action can help our planet?

A. Throwing rubbish into rivers.

B. Wasting water every day.

C. Planting trees.

Question 5：What is the main idea of the article?

A. Earth is our home, and we should take care of it.

B. All people live near the sea.

C. There are no plants in space.

## Answers

1\. **A. Blue and green.** Earth looks blue and green when we see it from space.

2\. **B. Oceans and seas.** The blue parts of Earth are oceans and seas.

3\. **C. To drink, cook, and wash.** Water is important for people in everyday life.

4\. **C. Planting trees.** Planting trees is one way to help take care of our planet.

5\. **A. Earth is our home, and we should take care of it.** The article explains why Earth is important and how people can help protect it.
