# The Helpful Robot

## Article

Mia has a small robot at home. Her father gives it to her on her birthday. The robot is white and blue, and it has two big eyes.

Mia calls the robot Toby. Toby can talk, play music, and answer simple questions. It can also help Mia with some small jobs.

Every morning, Toby tells Mia the time. It reminds her to eat breakfast and take her schoolbag. Mia sometimes forgets these things, so Toby is very helpful.

One afternoon, Mia comes home from school. She is tired and hungry. Toby plays her favorite song and tells her that lunch is on the table.

Mia smiles. “Thank you, Toby! You are a good friend.”

Later, Mia asks Toby to help her learn English words. Toby says a word, and Mia repeats it. They practice for twenty minutes.

Before bed, Mia says good night to Toby. She puts the robot on its charger.

“I will learn ten new words tomorrow,” Mia says.

Toby answers, “Good plan, Mia!”

## Questions

**Question 1：Who gives Mia the robot?**

A. Her mother.

B. Her father.

C. Her teacher.

**Question 2：What color is Toby?**

A. White and blue.

B. Red and yellow.

C. Black and green.

**Question 3：What does Toby remind Mia to do in the morning?**

A. Feed the cat.

B. Clean her room.

C. Eat breakfast and take her schoolbag.

**Question 4：What does Mia practice with Toby?**

A. English words.

B. Football skills.

C. Drawing pictures.

**Question 5：What does Mia plan to do tomorrow?**

A. Buy a new robot.

B. Learn ten new words.

C. Visit her grandmother.

## Answers

1\. **B. Her father.** Mia's father gives her the robot on her birthday.

2\. **A. White and blue.** Toby is a small robot with a white and blue body.

3\. **C. Eat breakfast and take her schoolbag.** Toby reminds Mia about these things every morning.

4\. **A. English words.** Toby says words, and Mia repeats them to practice her English.

5\. **B. Learn ten new words.** Mia tells Toby that she will learn ten new words the next day.
