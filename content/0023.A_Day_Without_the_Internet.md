# A Day Without the Internet

## Article

It is Sunday morning. Kevin wants to watch a funny video on his tablet, but the internet is not working.

He turns the tablet off and on again, but nothing changes.

“Dad, I cannot use the internet!” Kevin says.

His father looks at the tablet. “Maybe we can do something else today.”

Kevin feels bored at first. Then his father takes a ball from the garage.

“Would you like to play basketball with me?” Dad asks.

Kevin smiles. “Yes, let's go!”

They walk to the park near their home. The weather is warm, and many people are outside. Kevin and his father play basketball for an hour. Kevin gets tired, but he has a lot of fun.

After lunch, Kevin helps his mother make a fruit salad. He cuts some bananas and apples. His mother adds oranges and grapes.

In the evening, Kevin reads a comic book and talks with his family.

Before bed, he says, “Today is a good day. I don't need the internet to have fun!”

## Questions

**Question 1：What does Kevin want to do in the morning?**

A. Watch a video.

B. Read a newspaper.

C. Play the piano.

**Question 2：Why can't Kevin watch a video?**

A. His tablet is broken.

B. The internet is not working.

C. His father takes the tablet away.

**Question 3：What does Kevin do with his father?**

A. They play basketball.

B. They go shopping.

C. They ride their bikes.

**Question 4：What does Kevin help his mother make?**

A. A chocolate cake.

B. A bowl of soup.

C. A fruit salad.

**Question 5：How does Kevin feel about his day?**

A. He thinks it is boring.

B. He thinks it is a good day.

C. He wants to stay in bed all day.

## Answers

1\. **A. Watch a video.** Kevin wants to watch a funny video on his tablet.

2\. **B. The internet is not working.** He cannot watch the video because the internet does not work.

3\. **A. They play basketball.** Kevin and his father go to the park and play basketball together.

4\. **C. A fruit salad.** Kevin helps his mother prepare bananas, apples, oranges, and grapes.

5\. **B. He thinks it is a good day.** Kevin enjoys playing outside, helping his mother, and spending time with his family.
