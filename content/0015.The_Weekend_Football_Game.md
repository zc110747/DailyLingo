# The Weekend Football Game

## Article

Every Saturday, Daniel plays football with his friends. They meet at a park near their school.

Daniel is not the fastest player, but he is good at passing the ball. His friend Leo is very fast and likes to score goals.

This Saturday, the weather is sunny and warm. Ten boys come to the park for a game. They make two teams and start playing at nine o'clock.

At first, Daniel's team is behind. The other team scores two goals, but Daniel and his friends do not give up.

In the second half, Daniel passes the ball to Leo. Leo runs toward the goal and kicks the ball into the net. Everyone cheers!

A few minutes later, Daniel's team scores another goal. The game ends in a draw.

The boys are tired, but they are happy. They sit under a tree, drink water, and talk about the game.

Daniel learns that playing together is more important than winning every time.

## Questions

Question 1：When does Daniel play football with his friends?

A. Every Monday.

B. Every Saturday.

C. Every Sunday.

Question 2：What is Daniel good at?

A. Passing the ball.

B. Running very fast.

C. Being a goalkeeper.

Question 3：How many goals does the other team score at first?

A. One.

B. Three.

C. Two.

Question 4：Who scores a goal after Daniel passes the ball?

A. Leo.

B. Daniel's teacher.

C. A boy from the other team.

Question 5：How does the game end?

A. Daniel's team wins by three goals.

B. The game ends in a draw.

C. The other team leaves the park.

## Answers

1\. **B. Every Saturday.** Daniel and his friends meet at the park every Saturday to play football.

2\. **A. Passing the ball.** Daniel is good at passing, even though he is not the fastest player.

3\. **C. Two.** The other team scores two goals before Daniel's team begins to catch up.

4\. **A. Leo.** Daniel passes the ball to Leo, who runs toward the goal and scores.

5\. **B. The game ends in a draw.** Both teams score two goals, so neither team wins.
