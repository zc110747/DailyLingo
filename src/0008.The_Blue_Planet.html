The Blue Planet

Article

Earth is our home. It is a beautiful planet in space.

From space, Earth looks blue and green. The blue parts are oceans and seas. The green and brown parts are land.

There are many animals and plants on Earth. People live in cities, towns, and villages. Some people live near the sea, and others live near mountains.

Water is very important for life. People need water to drink, cook, and wash. Plants need water to grow, too.

The Earth is our home, and we need to take care of it. We can save water, plant trees, and keep our streets clean.

Even small actions can help make our planet a better place.
