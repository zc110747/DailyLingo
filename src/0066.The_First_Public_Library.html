The First Public Library

Many years ago, books were not easy for everyone to find. Some books were very expensive, and many people did not have books at home.

One day, a town leader had an idea. He wanted to open a public library. People could visit the library and read books without buying them.

The town built a small library near the town hall. There were books about history, science, animals, and stories. A librarian helped people find the books they wanted.

Soon, many children and adults visited the library. Some people read there, and others borrowed books to take home.

Today, public libraries are found in many towns and cities. People can read books, study, use computers, and join activities there.

Emma visits her local library every week. She likes reading stories about faraway places. She thinks a library is a wonderful place to learn.
