The School Garden

Mia's school has a small garden behind the library. This year, her class wants to grow vegetables.

On Monday, the students bring seeds, water, and small tools. Their teacher shows them how to put the seeds into the soil. They make small holes and cover the seeds with earth.

Every morning, two students water the garden. Mia checks the plants before her first lesson. At first, she sees only green leaves. After a few weeks, some tomatoes begin to grow.

The students are excited. They take care of the plants and keep the garden clean. One Friday, they pick the tomatoes and take them to the school kitchen.

The cook makes a fresh tomato soup for lunch. Everyone enjoys it.

Mia learns that plants need time, water, and care. She wants to grow carrots next year.
