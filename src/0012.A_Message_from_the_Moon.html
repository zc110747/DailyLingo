A Message from the Moon

Article

0:00 / --:-- 正在加载音频…

Good evening, everyone! Here is some exciting news from the science center.

A new robot is ready to travel to the Moon. Its name is Little Star. It is small, white, and round. It has two cameras and four wheels.

Little Star can take pictures of the Moon. It can also move over rocks and look for interesting places. Scientists want to learn more about the Moon's surface.

The robot does not need a driver. Scientists on Earth can control it with computers.

The trip will take several days. When Little Star arrives, it will send pictures back to Earth.

Many children are excited about this project. Some want to become scientists when they grow up.

Who knows? One day, you may help explore space, too!
