It is a sunny morning. A little bee flies out of its home in a tree. It is ready to look for flowers.

The bee flies over a green field. It sees many yellow and pink flowers. It lands on a yellow flower and drinks a little sweet nectar.

Then the bee flies to another flower. It moves from flower to flower and collects nectar. The bee is small, but it works very hard.

A little boy named Sam sees the bee in the garden. He wants to look at it, but he does not touch it.

“Look, Mom! The bee is busy,” Sam says.

His mother smiles. “Yes. Bees help flowers grow by moving pollen from one flower to another.”

Sam watches the bee for a few minutes. Then he sees a butterfly near a red flower.

“What a beautiful day!” he says.

In the afternoon, the bee flies back to its home. It has had a busy day in the garden.
