A new phone is coming to the town this week. The shop has a big poster in its window.

The poster says, “Meet the SmartOne! It is small, light, and easy to use. Take clear photos, listen to music, and talk to your friends. The battery lasts all day!”

Amy sees the poster on her way to school. She wants a new phone, but she does not have enough money yet.

After school, she visits the shop with her mother. A shop worker shows them the phone. Amy takes a photo of a flower and looks at it on the screen. The picture is bright and clear.

Her mother asks about the price. The phone costs two hundred dollars. Amy decides to save her money and buy it later.

At home, she puts some money into her small box. She is happy because she has a plan.
