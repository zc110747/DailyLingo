The Lion and the Mouse

One day, a big lion sleeps under a tree. A little mouse runs over his body. The lion wakes up and catches the mouse.

“Why are you on my body?” the lion asks.

“Please let me go,” says the mouse. “One day, I may help you.”

The lion laughs. “You are so small! How can you help me?” But he lets the mouse go.

A few days later, the lion walks into a hunter’s net. He cannot get out. He roars loudly. The little mouse hears him and runs to help.

The mouse bites the net with his sharp teeth. Soon, the net has a big hole, and the lion is free.

“Thank you,” says the lion. “I was wrong. Even a small animal can help a big one.”

The mouse smiles. They become good friends.

Moral: Do not judge others by their size. Everyone can help in some way.
