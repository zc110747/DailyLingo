The First Telephone Call

Many years ago, people did not have mobile phones. They could not send text messages or make video calls.

In 1876, Alexander Graham Bell made an important telephone call. He was an inventor. He worked with other people to make the telephone better.

The first telephone was very different from a phone today. It was big and simple. People could talk to someone in another room or place, but they could not see the other person.

Today, telephones are much smaller. People can call their friends, take photos, watch videos, and use the internet. Some phones can even help people find their way in a new city.

Phones make many things easier. However, people should not use them all day. It is also important to talk to family and friends face to face.

Technology changes our lives, but people are still important.
