What Happens at the Beach?

A beach is a place where the land meets the sea. Some beaches have soft white sand, while others have small stones or dark sand.

Many people visit beaches to swim, walk, or enjoy the sunshine. Children like building sandcastles and looking for small shells. Some people also enjoy surfing when the waves are good.

Have you ever noticed that the sea changes during the day? Sometimes the water moves far away from the land. At other times, it comes closer. This is called the tide.

Tides happen mainly because of the Moon's gravity. The Moon pulls on the water in the sea. The Sun also affects the tides, but its effect is smaller than the Moon's.

The beach is also home to many animals. Small crabs walk across the sand. Seabirds look for fish near the water. Some sea turtles come onto beaches to lay their eggs.

However, beaches need care. Plastic bags and bottles can hurt sea animals. People should take their rubbish home and keep the beach clean.

A beach is more than a place for fun. It is also an important home for many living things.
