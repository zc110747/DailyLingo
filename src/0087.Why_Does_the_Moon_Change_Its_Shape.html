Why Does the Moon Change Its Shape?

Look at the Moon in the night sky. Sometimes it looks like a big round ball. On other nights, it looks like a small light or a thin line. Does the Moon really change its shape?

No. The Moon is always round, like a ball. What changes is the part we can see from Earth.

The Moon does not make its own light. It reflects light from the Sun. Half of the Moon is always bright because sunlight shines on it.

As the Moon moves around Earth, we see different parts of its bright side. This makes the Moon look different on different nights.

There are four main stages of the Moon's cycle. We call them the new moon, first quarter, full moon, and last quarter.

During a new moon, we can hardly see the Moon. During a full moon, we can see its bright side as a large, round shape.

The Moon takes about 29.5 days to complete one cycle. This is why its appearance changes over time.

Next time you look at the night sky, try to find the Moon. Can you tell which stage it is in?
