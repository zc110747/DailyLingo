A Surprise Birthday Party

Today is Ben's birthday. He is twelve years old. He thinks it is a normal day, so he goes to school as usual.

After school, Ben walks home with his friend Mike. They talk about their favorite games and their plans for the weekend.

When Ben opens the front door, the lights are off. The house is very quiet.

Suddenly, the lights come on. His family and friends shout, “Happy birthday, Ben!”

Ben is surprised and happy. There are colorful balloons in the living room. A big chocolate cake is on the table, and there are many presents beside it.

Ben blows out the candles and makes a wish. Then everyone sings a birthday song and eats cake together.

After the party, Ben opens his presents. He gets a new book, a football, and a blue schoolbag.

“Thank you, everyone!” Ben says with a big smile. It is a wonderful birthday for him.
