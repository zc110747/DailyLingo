A Visit to the Library

Article

On Sunday morning, Emma goes to the library. She wants to find a book about animals.

The library is near her home. It is a quiet and beautiful place. Emma walks to the front desk and asks a woman for help.

“Where can I find books about animals?” Emma asks.

“They are on the second floor,” the woman says.

Emma takes the stairs and looks at the books. She finds a book about pandas. The book has many pictures and interesting facts.

Emma sits by the window and reads for thirty minutes. She learns that pandas eat bamboo every day.

Before she leaves, Emma borrows the book with her library card. She is happy because she has something new to read at home.
