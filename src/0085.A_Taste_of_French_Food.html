A Taste of French Food

France is famous for its food. Many people around the world enjoy French bread, cheese, cakes, and other dishes.

One popular food is the croissant. It is a small, soft pastry with many thin layers. People often eat it for breakfast with coffee or milk.

Another famous food is baguette. It is a long, thin loaf of bread with a crisp outside and a soft inside. People buy it from bakeries and use it to make sandwiches.

France also has many kinds of cheese. Some cheese is soft, and some is hard. People often eat cheese with bread or fruit.

For lunch or dinner, many French people enjoy dishes such as ratatouille. It is a warm vegetable dish with tomatoes, onions, and other vegetables. It is colorful and full of flavor.

French desserts are popular, too. Crème brûlée has a soft cream inside and a thin, hard layer of sugar on top. People break the sugar with a small spoon before eating it.

French food is not only about taste. It is also part of the country's culture. Food brings family and friends together around the table.
