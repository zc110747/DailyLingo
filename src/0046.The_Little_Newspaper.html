The Little Newspaper

Every Friday, a small newspaper comes to the homes in Green Town. It has news about the town, stories about people, and information about local events.

Emma is a young student. She likes reading the newspaper with her grandfather.

One Friday morning, her grandfather opens the newspaper at the breakfast table.

“Look, Emma!” he says. “There is a new park near the river.”

Emma reads the news. The park has a playground, a walking path, and many trees. It will open next Monday.

“That sounds wonderful!” Emma says. “Can we visit it after school?”

Her grandfather smiles. “Of course.”

On another page, Emma sees a short story about a boy who finds a lost dog. The boy takes the dog to an animal center, and the dog's family comes to get it.

Emma likes this story because the boy helps an animal.

Before breakfast ends, she finds a small advertisement. A bookshop near her school is having a book sale this weekend. Some books cost only two dollars.

Emma wants to buy a book about animals, so she writes the time and address in her notebook.

After breakfast, she takes the newspaper to her father.

“Dad, I found three interesting things today!” she says.

Her father asks, “Why do you like the newspaper?”

Emma thinks for a moment.

“It tells me what is happening around me,” she answers.

Her father nods. He says that reading the news can help people learn about their town and make good plans.

Emma puts on her school bag. She is already thinking about the new park and the book sale.
