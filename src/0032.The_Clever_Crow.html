The Clever Crow

It is a hot day in summer. A crow flies over a small village. It is very thirsty and wants to find some water.

The crow looks around. It sees a garden, a house, and a small road. Then it finds a bottle near a tree.

The crow looks inside the bottle. There is a little water at the bottom, but its beak cannot reach it.

The crow thinks for a moment. Then it sees some small stones on the ground.

The crow picks up a stone with its beak and drops it into the bottle. Then it picks up another stone. It does this again and again.

Slowly, the water rises in the bottle. At last, the crow can reach the water and drink it.

The crow feels happy and flies away.

A young girl watches the crow from her window. She tells her father what she sees.

“Animals can be very clever,” her father says.

The girl smiles. She learns that a small idea can solve a big problem.
