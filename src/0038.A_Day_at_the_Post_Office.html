A Day at the Post Office

Today is Friday. Sophie wants to send a birthday card to her grandmother. Her grandmother lives in another town.

After breakfast, Sophie writes a short message inside the card. She draws a small flower and writes, “Happy Birthday, Grandma! I hope you have a wonderful day.”

Then she puts the card into an envelope. She writes her grandmother's name and address on the front.

Sophie walks to the post office near her home. There are a few people waiting in line. She stands behind a man with a large box.

When it is her turn, Sophie gives the envelope to the worker.

“How much does it cost to send this card?” she asks.

The worker checks the envelope and tells her the price. Sophie pays the money and gets a stamp.

The worker puts the stamp on the envelope and places the card with the other letters.

“Your card is ready to go,” the worker says.

Sophie smiles and thanks her.

On the way home, Sophie thinks about how a small card can make someone feel loved. She cannot visit her grandmother this weekend, but she hopes the card will bring her a happy surprise.

That evening, her grandmother calls her.

“Thank you for the birthday card!” her grandmother says. “It made my day.”

Sophie is glad. She learns that a little message can mean a lot to someone far away.
