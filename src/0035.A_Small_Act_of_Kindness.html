A Small Act of Kindness

It is Monday morning. Ben is walking to school when he sees an old man near the road.

The man has two heavy bags. He wants to cross the road, but many cars are passing by.

Ben stops and asks, “Can I help you?”

The old man smiles. “Yes, please. These bags are very heavy.”

Ben takes one bag and walks with the man to the other side of the road. They wait for the traffic light to turn green before they cross.

“Thank you, young man,” says the old man. “You are very kind.”

Ben smiles and says, “You are welcome.”

The old man tells Ben that he is going to visit his daughter. She lives in a house near the school.

Ben looks at his watch. He still has enough time to get to school, so he walks with the man for a few more minutes.

When Ben arrives at school, he tells his teacher what happened.

“That was a good thing to do,” she says. “Small acts of kindness can make someone's day better.”

Ben feels happy. He does not help the man to get a prize. He helps because someone needs him.

That afternoon, Ben sees a new student sitting alone in the classroom. He walks over and asks, “Would you like to sit with us?”

The student smiles. “Yes, thank you!”

Ben learns that kindness does not need to be big. Sometimes, a little help means a lot.
