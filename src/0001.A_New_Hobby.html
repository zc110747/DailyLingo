A New Hobby

My name is David. I am thirty years old. I work in a small company.

Every day, I am busy with my work. Sometimes, I feel tired after work.

One day, I decide to learn something new. I want to learn how to cook.

On Saturday morning, I go to the supermarket. I buy some eggs, tomatoes, and rice.

In the afternoon, I cook my first meal. It is not perfect, but it tastes good!

Now, I cook every weekend. I feel happy because I can learn new things and take care of myself.
