The Day the River Rose

It rains all night in Green Town. In the morning, the river is much higher than usual. The water is close to the road.

Mr. Brown works at the town office. He sees the river and calls the local news station. Soon, a message is on the radio. It says, “Please stay away from the river today.”

Anna hears the message while eating breakfast. She tells her family about it. Her father moves their car to a safe place. Her mother puts some food and water in a bag.

At noon, the rain stops. The river is still high, but the water does not reach the houses. Some people help clean the streets.

In the evening, the town is quiet again. Anna is glad that everyone is safe. She learns that a simple message can help many people.
