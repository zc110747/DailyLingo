A Little Coffee Shop

There is a small coffee shop on Green Street. It is next to a flower shop and across from a bus stop.

The coffee shop opens at seven o'clock every morning. It sells coffee, tea, milk, and fresh bread.

The shop has four small tables and eight chairs. There are flowers on the tables, and the walls are light yellow.

A cup of coffee costs three dollars. A cup of tea costs two dollars. People can also buy a sandwich for four dollars.

Many people come here before work. They sit by the window, drink their coffee, and read the news.

The owner is a friendly woman named Anna. She always smiles and says hello to her customers.

The coffee shop is small, but many people like it because it is warm and comfortable.
