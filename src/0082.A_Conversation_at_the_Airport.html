Anna: Excuse me. Is this the line for check-in?

Man: Yes, it is. Are you flying to London?

Anna: Yes. My flight leaves at ten thirty. What time is it now?

Man: It is nine fifteen. You have enough time.

Anna: Good. Where can I find the check-in desk?

Man: It is over there, next to the coffee shop.

Anna: Thank you. Do I need to show my passport?

Man: Yes. You need your passport and your ticket.

Anna: Here they are. Is this my boarding pass?

Man: Yes. Your gate is number twelve.

Anna: Where is gate twelve?

Man: Go straight and turn left. You will see the signs.

Anna: Great! Thank you for your help.

Man: You're welcome. Have a nice flight!
