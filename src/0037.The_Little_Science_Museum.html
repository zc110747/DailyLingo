The Little Science Museum

On Sunday, Oliver visits a science museum with his sister, Lily. They both like learning about the world.

At the entrance, a large sign says, “Discover Something New!”

First, they go to a room about space. A big model of the Sun stands in the middle of the room. Around it, there are models of the Earth and other planets.

Lily looks at the Earth and says, “Our planet looks so small!”

Oliver reads a sign about the Moon. He learns that the Moon moves around the Earth.

Next, they visit a room about the human body. There is a large model of a heart. A guide explains that the heart pumps blood around the body.

Oliver listens carefully. He thinks it is amazing that his heart works all day and all night.

Before they leave, the children visit a small science shop. Lily buys a book about stars, and Oliver buys a simple science kit.

At home, Oliver opens the box. It has a small light, some wires, and a battery. He follows the instructions and makes the light turn on.

“Look, Lily! I made it work!” he says.

His sister smiles. They decide to do another science activity together next weekend.

Oliver learns that science is not only in books. People can discover many things by looking, asking questions, and trying new ideas.
