The Missing Recipe

It is Sunday afternoon. Noah wants to make a cake for his mother's birthday. He finds a large bowl, some flour, two eggs, and a little milk in the kitchen.

But there is one problem. He cannot find the cake recipe.

Noah looks in a drawer and under a book on the table. He also checks his phone, but he does not see the recipe there.

His older sister, Emma, comes into the kitchen.

“What are you looking for?” she asks.

“I want to make a cake, but I cannot find the recipe,” Noah says.

Emma thinks for a moment. Then she remembers something. Their grandmother sent them a recipe last year. She looks through some old messages on her phone and finds it.

“Here it is!” Emma says.

The recipe says to mix the flour, eggs, milk, and sugar in a bowl. Then they need to put the mixture into a cake pan and bake it in the oven.

Noah and Emma work together. Noah mixes the ingredients, and Emma prepares the cake pan.

After some time, a sweet smell comes from the oven. The cake is ready!

They let it cool and put some fruit on top. When their mother comes home, Noah brings the cake to the table.

“Happy birthday!” he says.

Their mother smiles and gives them both a big hug.

Noah is happy. He learns that working together can make a difficult job easier.
