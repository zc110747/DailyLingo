Ben is a young boy. He likes reading the town newspaper with his grandfather every Sunday.

One morning, Ben sees an interesting story on the front page. A small school garden has a lot of new vegetables. The students grow tomatoes, carrots, and beans. They work in the garden after class.

Ben wants to see the garden. He and his grandfather walk to the school. A teacher welcomes them and shows them the plants. Some students water the vegetables, and others take out small weeds.

“We want to give some vegetables to older people in our town,” says a student. “We are happy to help them.”

Ben thinks this is a great idea. He wants to start a small garden at home, too. His grandfather smiles and says, “We can plant some tomatoes next weekend.”

Ben is excited. He knows that a small garden can do something good for other people.
