The History of Cheese

Cheese is one of the oldest foods in the world. People have made cheese for thousands of years. It is made from milk, usually from cows, goats, or sheep.

No one knows exactly who made the first cheese. A long time ago, people carried milk in bags made from animal skin. Sometimes, the milk became thick because of natural changes. People discovered that this thick milk could become a kind of food. This may have been one of the first steps in making cheese.

Cheese became popular in many ancient countries. In ancient Egypt, people made and ate cheese. The Greeks and Romans also enjoyed it. Roman people made different kinds of cheese and took them to other places.

Over time, people in different countries developed their own cheeses. France became famous for soft cheese, while Italy became famous for hard cheese. Cheddar cheese became popular in England. Each kind of cheese has its own taste, smell, and shape.

Today, cheese is made in many ways. Some cheese is soft and easy to spread. Other cheese is hard and can be cut into small pieces. People put cheese on bread, pizza, sandwiches, and many other foods.

Cheese is now enjoyed around the world. A simple food made thousands of years ago has become an important part of many food cultures.
