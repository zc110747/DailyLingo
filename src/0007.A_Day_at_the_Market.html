A Day at the Market

Article

0:00 / --:-- 正在加载音频…

On Sunday morning, Lucy goes to the market with her mother. They need to buy some food for lunch.

The market is busy. Many people are buying fruit, vegetables, and meat. Lucy sees a man selling fresh apples.

“Can we buy some apples?” Lucy asks.

“Of course,” her mother says.

They buy six red apples and some bananas. Then they go to a vegetable shop. Lucy helps her mother choose some carrots and potatoes.

At the next shop, her mother buys some fish. Lucy carries a small bag, and her mother carries the other bags.

On the way home, Lucy sees a little dog near a tree. The dog looks hungry. Lucy gives it a small piece of bread.

When they get home, Lucy helps her mother wash the vegetables. She is happy because she helps her family today.
