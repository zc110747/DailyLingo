Helping a Neighbor

Article

0:00 / --:-- 正在加载音频…

It is Saturday morning. Tom is at home. He is reading a book when he hears a noise outside.

He looks out of the window. His neighbor, Mrs. Green, is carrying two heavy bags. She is an old woman, and the bags are difficult for her to carry.

Tom goes outside and says, “Good morning, Mrs. Green! Can I help you?”

Mrs. Green smiles and says, “Yes, please. Thank you, Tom!”

Tom takes one bag and walks with her to her house. The bag is full of fruit and vegetables.

When they arrive, Mrs. Green says, “You are very kind. Would you like some apples?”

Tom smiles and says, “Yes, please! Thank you!”

He takes two apples and goes home. He feels happy because he has helped someone.
