A Perfect Weekend at the Botanical Garden

Saturday is coming, but Emma has no plans. She does not want to stay at home all day. She wants to find a nice place to relax.

Her friend Lucy has an idea. "Let's visit the botanical garden!" she says.

The garden is not far from the city. They take a bus and arrive at ten in the morning.

First, they walk along a quiet path. There are many beautiful flowers and tall trees. Some birds are singing in the trees, and butterflies fly over the flowers.

Next, they find a small lake. There are ducks swimming in the water. Emma takes some photos, while Lucy sits on a bench and reads a book.

At noon, they have a picnic under a big tree. They eat sandwiches, fruit, and drink some water.

In the afternoon, they visit a small plant shop near the garden. Emma buys a little green plant for her bedroom.

Before going home, they look at the lake one more time. It is a simple day, but both friends feel relaxed and happy.

Sometimes, the best weekend plan is not an expensive trip. A beautiful place and a good friend are enough.
