Why Do Cities Have Street Names?

Have you ever looked at a street sign? It may show a name like King Street or River Road. These names help people find places.

Long ago, many streets did not have clear names. People often used trees, shops, or other places to give directions. This could be difficult when a town grew bigger.

Today, street names help people travel around a city. They also help drivers, taxi workers, and people who deliver food and letters.

Some streets are named after famous people. Others are named after rivers, trees, or old buildings. A street name can tell us something about the history of a place.

Street names are important in an emergency, too. If someone needs a doctor or an ambulance, a clear street name helps people find the right place quickly.

Next time you walk down a street, look at its sign. Its name may have an interesting story behind it!
