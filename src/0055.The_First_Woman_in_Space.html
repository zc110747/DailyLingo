Many years ago, people wanted to travel into space. They wanted to see the Earth from above and learn more about the stars.

In 1963, a woman named Valentina Tereshkova traveled into space. She was from the Soviet Union. She was the first woman in space.

Valentina was not a famous scientist before her space trip. She worked in a factory and loved flying. Later, she learned many new things to prepare for her journey.

Her trip lasted almost three days. She traveled around the Earth many times. From her spacecraft, she could see the blue Earth and the dark sky.

Many people were excited about her journey. She showed that women could also take part in space travel.

Today, people still study space. They use rockets, computers, and special machines. Some people hope to travel to the Moon or Mars one day.

Valentina's story reminds us that people can do amazing things when they work hard and learn new skills.
