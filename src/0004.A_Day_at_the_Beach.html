A Day at the Beach

Article

0:00 / --:-- 正在加载音频…

It is Sunday. Lucy and her family go to the beach. The weather is sunny, and the sky is blue.

Lucy wears a yellow hat and a blue dress. She takes a small bag with her. There are some sandwiches, two apples, and a bottle of water in the bag.

When they arrive at the beach, Lucy sees many people. Some people are swimming. Some children are playing with balls. A little boy is building a sandcastle.

Lucy takes off her shoes and walks on the sand. The sand is warm. She feels happy.

At noon, Lucy and her family sit under a tree and eat lunch. After lunch, they walk along the beach and look for shells.

In the afternoon, the weather becomes a little cloudy. Lucy and her family go home.

“What a lovely day!” Lucy says. She is tired, but she is very happy.
