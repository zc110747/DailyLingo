One morning, Daniel walks to the bus stop. On the way, he sees a brown wallet on the ground.

He picks it up and opens it carefully. There is some money, a bus card, and a small photo inside. Daniel does not know who owns the wallet.

He looks around, but nobody is there. Then he sees a police station across the street. He walks there and gives the wallet to a police officer.

The officer asks Daniel where he found it. Daniel explains everything. The officer writes down his name and says, “Thank you for being honest.”

That afternoon, a woman comes to the police station. She has lost her wallet and is very worried. The officer gives it back to her. She checks the money and photo, and everything is there.

The woman is happy. She asks the officer for Daniel's name so she can thank him.

The next day, she finds Daniel at the bus stop and thanks him. Daniel smiles. He is glad that he did the right thing.
