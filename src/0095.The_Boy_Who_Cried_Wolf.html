The Boy Who Cried Wolf

A long time ago, a young boy watched sheep on a hill. His job was to keep the sheep safe.

One day, the boy felt bored. He wanted to have some fun. So he shouted, “Wolf! Wolf! A wolf is coming!”

The people in the village heard him. They ran up the hill to help. But there was no wolf. The boy laughed at them.

A few days later, he did it again. “Wolf! Wolf!” he shouted. The villagers came to help, but once again, there was no wolf.

One afternoon, a real wolf came to the hill. The boy was very afraid. He shouted, “Wolf! Please help me!”

The villagers heard him, but they did not come. They thought he was telling another lie. The wolf scared the sheep away.

The boy felt very sorry. He learned that people may not believe you if you often tell lies.

Moral: Always tell the truth. If you lie too often, people may stop trusting you.
