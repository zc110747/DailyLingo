A Poster in the Town

One morning, Emma sees a colorful poster near the bus stop. It is about a book festival in the town library.

The festival is on Saturday. There are many activities for children and adults. People can read books, listen to stories, and meet local writers.

Emma likes reading, so she takes a photo of the poster. She sends it to her friend Jack.

“Would you like to go with me?” she asks.

“Of course!” Jack replies. “I want to find a book about space.”

On Saturday morning, they meet outside the library. The building is full of people. A woman reads a funny story to a group of children. A man talks about his new book.

Emma finds a book about animals, and Jack finds a book about the stars. They both buy their books and sit in the garden to read.

Before they leave, they see a small sign. It says, “Books can open new doors.”

Emma smiles. She thinks it is a good message. A book can take people to new places without a ticket.
