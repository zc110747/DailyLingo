Grandma’s Secret Recipe

Emma visits her grandmother every Sunday. Her grandmother is a very good cook. She can make many delicious dishes.

One Sunday, Emma wants to learn how to make a cake. Her grandmother smiles and takes out a big bowl.

First, they put some flour, sugar, eggs, and milk into the bowl. Then Emma mixes everything together. Her grandmother puts the mixture into the oven.

“Now we need to wait,” Grandma says.

Emma sits at the table and reads a book. Thirty minutes later, a sweet smell comes from the kitchen. The cake is ready!

Emma takes a small piece and tries it. It is soft and sweet.

“This is wonderful!” Emma says. “Can you teach me another recipe next Sunday?”

“Of course!” Grandma answers with a smile.
