George Washington was one of the most important people in American history. He was born on February 22, 1732, in Virginia. When he was young, he enjoyed outdoor activities and learned how to work on farms. He did not receive a university education, but he became a skilled leader and military officer.

In the 1750s, Washington joined the British army in North America. At that time, Britain controlled thirteen colonies along the east coast of North America. Many colonists were unhappy because the British government collected taxes from them, but they had little power to make decisions.

In 1775, the American Revolutionary War began. The thirteen colonies wanted to become independent from Britain. Washington was chosen to lead the Continental Army. This was a difficult job because his soldiers often lacked food, clothing, and weapons. They also had to fight against a powerful British army.

After several years of fighting, the American forces won the war with help from France. In 1783, Britain officially recognized American independence. Washington became a national hero because of his leadership during the war.

In 1789, Washington became the first president of the United States. He helped establish important traditions for the new government. For example, he created a cabinet to advise him and supported the idea that the president should not have unlimited power.

Washington served two terms as president. Although some people wanted him to remain in office longer, he decided to leave after eight years. This decision became an important example for future American presidents.

In 1799, Washington died at his home in Virginia. Today, he is remembered as the first president and one of the founders of the United States. His face also appears on the one-dollar bill and the quarter-dollar coin.

Washington's life shows how leadership, responsibility, and difficult decisions can influence the future of a country.
