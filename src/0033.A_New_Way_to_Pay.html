A New Way to Pay

Lucy goes to a small shop after school. She wants to buy a bottle of milk and a sandwich.

At the shop, she takes the food to the counter. The shop owner smiles and tells her the price.

Lucy opens her bag, but she cannot find her wallet. She feels worried.

“Can I pay with my phone?” she asks.

“Yes, you can,” says the shop owner.

Lucy takes out her phone and opens a payment app. She scans a code on the counter. Then she checks the price and touches the pay button.

A few seconds later, the phone shows a message: “Payment successful!”

Lucy smiles and thanks the shop owner. She takes her food and leaves the shop.

On her way home, she thinks about how technology changes daily life. Paying with a phone is easy, but people still need to check the price before they pay.

When Lucy gets home, she tells her brother about her experience. He says, “Technology is useful when we use it carefully.”

Lucy agrees. She puts her phone in her bag and enjoys her sandwich.
