The Day the Internet Went Away

Article

0:00 / --:-- 正在加载音频…

Last Saturday, Mia woke up and picked up her phone. She wanted to watch a video, but nothing worked. The internet was down.

Mia felt unhappy. She could not watch videos, read the news, or send messages to her friends. She tried to turn her phone off and on, but it did not help.

Her mother smiled and said, “Why not spend the day outside?”

Mia went to the park with her younger brother. They saw ducks on the lake and watched children play football. Later, they bought some bread and ate lunch under a tree.

In the afternoon, Mia visited her grandmother. They made a cake together. Her grandmother told her stories about her childhood. Mia laughed and asked many questions.

That evening, the internet started working again. Mia picked up her phone, but she did not use it at once. She sat with her family and played a card game.

Before bed, Mia wrote in her diary. She said that a day without the internet was not so bad. She learned that there were many fun things to do offline.
