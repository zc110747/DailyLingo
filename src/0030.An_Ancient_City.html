An Ancient City

Many years ago, people built a beautiful city near a river. The city had high walls, large gates, and many small houses.

People went to the market every morning. They bought rice, vegetables, fruit, and meat. Some people made clothes, and others made tools. Traders came from far away to sell their goods.

The river was very important to the city. People used its water for drinking, cooking, and washing. They also used boats to carry food and other things.

Today, the old city is no longer full of people. Some houses are gone, but parts of the walls are still there. Visitors come to see the old buildings and learn about life in the past.

A guide tells visitors stories about the people who lived there. Children look at old bowls, coins, and tools in a small museum.

The city teaches us something important. People lived differently in the past, but they also worked, traded, and cared for their families.

History helps us understand how people lived long ago.
