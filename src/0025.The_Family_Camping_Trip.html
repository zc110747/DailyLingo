The Family Camping Trip

On Saturday morning, Jack and his family go camping in the mountains. They put a tent, some food, and warm clothes in their car.

After an hour, they arrive at a beautiful place near a small lake. The air is fresh, and the trees are green. Jack helps his father put up the tent while his mother prepares lunch.

They eat sandwiches, eggs, and fruit under a big tree. After lunch, Jack walks around the lake with his sister, Lucy. They see some small fish in the water and hear birds in the trees.

In the afternoon, the family plays games and takes photos. Jack finds a smooth stone near the lake. He puts it in his pocket as a little memory of the trip.

When the sun goes down, they sit near their tent and look at the sky. Jack can see many bright stars.

“It is so beautiful here!” he says.

That night, Jack sleeps in the tent with his family. He feels tired but happy. He wants to go camping again soon.
