A new robot works in a hospital. Its name is Robin. It is small and has two wheels. It can move through the halls and carry small boxes.

Every morning, Robin helps the nurses. It takes medicine and clean towels to different rooms. The nurses can spend more time caring for patients because Robin does some simple jobs.

One day, a little boy sees Robin near his room. He asks, “Can you play with me?”

Robin cannot play games, but it can make a funny sound. The boy laughs, and his mother smiles.

A nurse tells the boy that Robin is a helpful machine. It cannot replace doctors or nurses, but it can make their work easier.

The boy waves goodbye when Robin leaves the room. He hopes to see it again tomorrow.
