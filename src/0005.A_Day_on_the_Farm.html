A Day on the Farm

Article

0:00 / --:-- 正在加载音频…

It is a beautiful Saturday. Anna and her brother Jack visit their uncle's farm. The farm is in the countryside, far from the city. When they arrive, their uncle welcomes them with a big smile. He shows them around the farm.

First, Anna sees three cows near a tree. The cows are eating grass. She gives them some water.

Then Jack sees a group of chickens. They are walking around the farm and looking for food. One chicken has a small yellow chick. Jack thinks it is very cute.

After that, their uncle takes them to see two horses. The horses are brown and white. Anna wants to ride a horse, but she is a little afraid.

“Don't worry,” her uncle says. “I can help you.”

Anna gets on the horse slowly. Her uncle walks beside her. Soon, she feels more comfortable and begins to smile.

In the afternoon, Anna and Jack help their uncle pick some apples. They put the apples in a big basket.

Before they leave, their uncle gives them some fresh eggs and apples to take home.

“Thank you for a wonderful day!” Anna says.

On the way home, Jack says, “I want to visit the farm again!”

Anna agrees. They have learned a lot about farm animals, and they have had a great time together.
