A Day Without the Internet

It is Sunday morning. Kevin wants to watch a funny video on his tablet, but the internet is not working.

He turns the tablet off and on again, but nothing changes.

“Dad, I cannot use the internet!” Kevin says.

His father looks at the tablet. “Maybe we can do something else today.”

Kevin feels bored at first. Then his father takes a ball from the garage.

“Would you like to play basketball with me?” Dad asks.

Kevin smiles. “Yes, let's go!”

They walk to the park near their home. The weather is warm, and many people are outside. Kevin and his father play basketball for an hour. Kevin gets tired, but he has a lot of fun.

After lunch, Kevin helps his mother make a fruit salad. He cuts some bananas and apples. His mother adds oranges and grapes.

In the evening, Kevin reads a comic book and talks with his family.

Before bed, he says, “Today is a good day. I don't need the internet to have fun!”
