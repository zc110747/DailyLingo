A Trip on the Ferry

On Sunday morning, David goes to the island with his family. They travel by ferry because there is no bridge between the island and the city.

At the port, David sees many people waiting for the boat. Some people carry bags, and others take photos of the sea. David buys a ticket for himself and sits next to his sister.

The ferry leaves the port at nine o'clock. David stands near a window and watches the water. He sees a few small boats and some white birds flying above the sea.

After forty minutes, the ferry arrives at the island. The family walks to a small beach and has lunch under a tree. David plays with his sister in the sand.

In the afternoon, they visit a little shop and buy some gifts for their friends. They take the ferry home before sunset.

David is tired but happy. He wants to visit the island again.
