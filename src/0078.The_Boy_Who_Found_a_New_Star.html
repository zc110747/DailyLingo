The Boy Who Found a New Star

One night, a boy named Leo looks at the sky with his small telescope. He loves stars and planets. Every evening, he spends time in his garden and watches the night sky.

Leo sees many bright stars. He writes their names in a notebook. One night, he sees a small light near a group of stars. He does not remember seeing it before.

He looks again the next night. The small light is still there. Leo takes a picture and sends it to a science club at a local university.

A few days later, a scientist writes back. She says the light is not a new star. It is a planet that Leo has never noticed before.

Leo feels a little disappointed, but the scientist tells him that his careful work is important. He has found something new to him and asked a good question.

The next week, Leo visits the university. The scientist shows him a larger telescope and explains how people study space.

Leo learns that science takes time, patience, and careful work. He goes home with a new dream. One day, he hopes to become a scientist.
